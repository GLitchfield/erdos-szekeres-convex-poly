# Ticket 0033: build and falsification-test Research Factory v1

## Question

Can a bounded, reproducible question factory generate a broad pool of typed
mathematical questions, reject circular or strategically inert variants, and
return a small number of literal research cards whose answers would change the
estimated work on `D-HALF`, `D-DECOMP`, or `D-NORMALIZE`?

This is a research-system experiment under `FP-14`.  It is not a mathematical
route and cannot change the proof ledger.  Ticket 0032 closed without a
proof-ready exact-route card: Pascal lacing retained a producer gap, the
multi-anchor atlas could not freeze a noncircular certificate, and the mutation
source card failed its own deletion-discrimination rule.  V1 therefore tests a
different process: high-throughput typed generation followed by severe
correlation, premise, model-tier, falsifiability, and return-bridge filters.

## Frozen scope

The funnel counts are workload ceilings, not validity evidence:

```text
720--1,200 typed microquestions
  -> measured correlated mechanism clusters, with no diversity claim from count
  -> at most 72 literal research cards
  -> at most 24 bounded investigations
  -> at most 8 synthesis candidates
  -> at most 3 proposals for a later proof slate.
```

The raw objects are hypotheses, not claimed good ideas.  Honest severe filter
attrition is an informative outcome rather than generator failure, and no
minimum survivor count is imposed.  A literal card must state an exact theorem
or falsifier, its model tier, typed source and consumer edges, missing return
bridge, cheapest test, correlated family, negative evidence, and
counterfactual value.  No Lean proof begins in v1.

## Pre-generation conformance gate

Before any lane is dispatched, root freezes the canonicalizer, hard-filter
rules, family classifier, audit sample, and their hashes.  A blind labeled
benchmark includes accepted historical theorem cards and at least one example
of each critical defect: target-equivalent premise, stored witness or selector,
producer gap, model-tier slippage, strategically inert true statement, known
counterexample, deletion-undiscriminated field, duplicate completed result,
and operational resource stop.  The benchmark labels are withheld from the
classifier and auditors until both independent classifications terminate.

The conformance gate passes only if both classifiers agree, every critical
defect is detected with its correct class, no accepted historical card is
rejected as a critical defect, and every deliberate corruption is
discriminated.  Failure is `RFV1-CONFORMANCE-FAIL`; no question generation then
occurs.  This gate validates the instrument, not any generated mathematics.

## Immutable substrate

Root owns four machine-readable ledgers under the ignored run directory:

1. a theorem-interface graph of existing compiling declarations;
2. the load-bearing Wayfinder dependency graph;
3. a negative-knowledge ledger of falsifiers, expired interfaces, and shared
   family streaks; and
4. an ontology of objects, operations, invariants, relations, model tiers, and
   conclusion templates.

The model tiers are planar point configurations, realizable order types,
abstract order types/signotopes/oriented matroids, and combinatorial
relaxations.  A cross-tier card must name the realization or transfer theorem
it still needs.

## Generation slate

V1 has zero proof-producing slots and seven information-gathering packets in
sequential waves.  At most three specialists run concurrently and at most one
packet owns executable source.

Three independent generation lanes each target 240--400 raw questions:

- proof-graph generation, working backward from exact dependencies and forward
  from compiling interfaces;
- representation and geometry generation, including order types, allowable
  sequences, oriented matroids, Gale/Radon machinery, realization spaces,
  mutation complexes, and higher-dimensional projections; and
- transferred-machinery generation, including hypergraph/VC/fractional,
  entropy/compression, semialgebraic, optimization, topological, and stability
  mechanisms.

The twelve lenses receive 60--100 raw questions apiece.  No correlated
mathematical family may exceed 15 percent of the raw pool, and at least 25
percent of the pool must lie outside current portfolio families.  These are
throughput and breadth checks, not quality evidence.  A renamed descendant
inherits the evidence streak of its mechanism family.

## Hard filters and promotion

Reject a question immediately if the destination or an equivalent witness is
present in its premises, its model tier is ambiguous, it lacks a credible
consumer or return bridge, it aliases an expired interface without new
mathematics, a known counterexample applies, no bounded falsifier exists, or
knowing its answer would not change estimated remaining proof work.

The pre-frozen deterministic normalization supplies structural fingerprints.
Every literal card receives conclusion erasure, field-by-field premise
deletion, witness-provenance, and target-occurrence tests.  Every bridge edge
must name its source and consumer node, the mathematical model tier at both
ends, its evidence level, and a bounded test that can invalidate the edge.

Independent reviewers audit every promoted card plus a pre-frozen 20 percent
sample of all clustering and rejection decisions; the sample seed is frozen
before generation.  Selection uses a Pareto frontier over leverage, premise
independence, novelty, bounded falsifiability, cross-model robustness,
composability, and return-bridge specificity; a scalar rank alone cannot
promote a card.  Here cross-model robustness means transfer among mathematical
model tiers, never agreement among AI providers.

Stagewise diversity is mandatory: no family may exceed 25 percent of literal
cards or investigations; a 24-card investigation set must contain at least
eight independent unresolved producers; and at most two investigated cards may
share the same unresolved producer.  A synthesis candidate may combine
families, but no two final candidates may have the same complete dependency
DAG.  These checks prevent sacrificial raw diversity from disappearing before
promotion.

Up to 24 investigations may use exact small-model checks, symbolic implication
checks, existing API matching, counterexample replay, and primary-source
novelty review.  Every computation freezes its input and two independent
semantic checks before outcomes are read.  Finite survival never establishes a
uniform theorem.

The final synthesis graph searches only typed compositions of two to four
cards.  A promotion card must have an exact theorem contract, explicit
source/intermediate/consumer signatures, a premise-provenance audit, a named
strict decrease in unresolved obligations, a cheapest falsifier, a literature
classification, and an independent adversarial review.

## Decision criteria

- `RFV1-CONFORMANCE-FAIL`: the pre-generation classifier/filter benchmark
  fails; no generation occurs.
- `RFV1-PIPELINE-VALID-CANDIDATES`: the pipeline passes and yields one to three
  promotion cards.
- `RFV1-PIPELINE-VALID-NO-SURVIVOR`: the pipeline passes but no card merits a
  proof slate.
- `RFV1-GENERATION-SHORTFALL`: a lane fails its 60-question-per-lens throughput
  floor or its output is structurally invalid.
- `RFV1-FILTER-ATTRITION`: the full raw pool is valid but severe frozen filters
  leave fewer cards than the investigation capacity; this is an informative,
  pipeline-valid terminal rather than a failure.
- `RFV1-GENERATION-CONCENTRATED`: the raw pool is schema-valid but violates a
  pre-frozen family or outside-portfolio breadth constraint.
- `RFV1-CHECKER-DISAGREEMENT`: independent semantic audits materially differ.
- `RFV1-RESOURCE-STOP`: the wall, CPU, memory, or output cap is reached.

All outcomes have neutral proof movement.  Evidence may be proposal or checked
computation.  Difficulty is not smaller unless a later compiling theorem
closes a named dependency.  Durable integration is restricted to this ticket,
`FP-14`, and negative knowledge; the raw question ocean remains transient.  Any
genuinely new portfolio family requires the standard separate Sol/Fable
admission review before it enters the registry.

## Resolution

The run terminated `RFV1-CHECKER-DISAGREEMENT` on 2026-08-18.  The blind
24-case conformance benchmark passed: two independently authored classifiers
agreed on every class and fired-rule trace, recalled every frozen critical
defect, and falsely rejected no accepted historical card.  Generation then
produced 720 schema-valid cards, exactly 60 per lens, with 720 structural
fingerprints and 484 cards outside the existing portfolio.  The same two
frozen classifiers agreed on all generated records, accepted 486, and the
frozen Pareto selector retained 72 cards spanning 40 declared families and 61
declared unresolved producers.

The independent semantic audit invalidated that apparent success.  Three
disjoint auditors reviewed all 72 selected cards plus a frozen 20 percent pool
sample, for 205 unique reviews.  The sampled decisions contained 28 material
mismatches among 144 cards (19.44 percent), exceeding the predeclared 10
percent systematic-failure threshold.  Every audit stratum exceeded that
threshold: `10/45`, `6/45`, and `12/54`.  Twelve of the 72 selected cards were
also materially misclassified.  Across the audited union, all 39 material
mismatches had been declared `ACCEPTED_LITERAL_CARD`: 18 duplicated completed
contracts, eight used target-equivalent premises, four retained producer gaps,
three stored a selector or witness, two lacked a falsifiable return bridge, two
were strategically inert, one crossed model tiers without a return, and one
ignored a known counterexample.

The frozen reconciler therefore released zero eligible cards even though 60
would have remained after local card-by-card corrections.  It performed no
backfill.  Investigations, synthesis, literature promotion, and Lean proof work
were skipped at the predeclared boundary.  The run used zero proof-producing
assignments.  Its three operational waves used nine separately frozen
information-gathering specialist assignments rather than the ticket's coarse
seven-packet forecast; the extra separation preserved blind conformance and
three-way audit independence and is recorded as a bookkeeping deviation.

**Net proof movement:** neutral.  **Evidence:** checked computation for the
structural pipeline and proposal-level independent semantic audits; no card
statement is proved.  **Difficulty displaced:** none.  `D-HALF`, `D-DECOMP`,
and `D-NORMALIZE` retain exactly their prior obligations.  **Counterfactual:**
knowing the result in advance would prevent investigation effort on these
cards and lower confidence in this factory design, but would not shorten an
exact proof.  **Planning consequence:** retain a research-factory child under
`FP-14` as gated, promote no v1 card, and require a fresh adversarial route
review before any v2.  A reviewed v2 must reconstruct semantic facts from the
theorem, dependency, and negative-knowledge graphs rather than trusting
generator fields; pass a frozen canary audit at or below 10 percent material
mismatch; and prevent selection from collapsing onto novelty-heavy lanes.
Ticket 0032 and this run are two consecutive information-only question-selection
blocks, so no descendant receives automatic promotion.  No load-bearing
destination edge changed.
