## Question

Can exact decomposition colors consume either the full six-column residual-cup
plateau with two foreign disjoint caps, or one of the two localized escaping
residual cups, to produce a forbidden target or a named strict predecessor?

## Frozen inputs

- `MinimumColumnPlateauTwoForeignCaps578` and its compiling constructor;
- the lower- and upper-localized residual escape packages;
- the disjoint universal-column residual-cup branch;
- all six disjoint cap blocks and the exact 32/23 residual density; and
- the relaxed all-base and exact-small projected countermodels recorded in
  Experiments 0079, 0082, and 0085.

## Decision criteria

- A plateau consumer must use the two foreign cap blocks and a triple-color or
  decomposition law; address/cardinality reasoning alone is expired.
- A localized consumer must use the escaping cup and all six columns, or name
  a strict predecessor with every inherited avoidance proved.
- Keep the upstream `hone` qualification explicit; either discharge it or
  open a separate multi-spine branch before calling the four-way package
  canonical for every 32-point configuration.
- Test a precise consumer against the relaxed full-matrix model before Lean
  implementation, then against exact-small decomposable models where its
  hypotheses make sense.
- Do not infer that state `(5,7,8)` is false from a projected or incidence-only
  model.

## Candidate outcomes

1. A compiling consumer closes the exact color-dual singleton gate.
2. A compiling theorem merges or eliminates at least one remaining branch and
   leaves a strictly sharper named obstruction.
3. A full-premise exact coloring refutes the proposed consumer; expire this
   matrix route without claiming the hereditary state false.

## Resolution

Open. A read-only exact-small audit expires the proposed local consumer using
only the plateau cup, its source cap, and two foreign caps: deterministic
15-vertex decomposable models reportedly realize every plateau placement
profile without a target. This is proposal-level computation, not a Lean
counterexample, and the models omit the full six-column/32-point density
premises. Therefore none of Outcomes 1--3 is reached. The next positive
consumer must use global information absent from those controls, such as all
six columns, the 24 deletion rows, full residual density, or a collective star
law. The `hone` qualification and multi-spine carrier branch remain explicit.
