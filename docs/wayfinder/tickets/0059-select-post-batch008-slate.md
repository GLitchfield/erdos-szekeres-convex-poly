# Ticket 0059: select the post-Batch-008 execution slate

## Question

Which currently executable portfolio items should form the next temporary
execution slate after Batch 008's five proposals all remain ledger-only?

## Decision criteria

Selection changes no portfolio membership or state and authorizes neither
detailed planning nor execution. Each slot must have a fixed mathematical
object, named consumer, bounded outcome branches, evidence gate, stop rule,
and stable mechanism family. Four is a capacity target rather than a quota.
Do not schedule suspended work, ledger-only factory proposals, undefined
hypothesis invention, larger instances, or inert computation merely to fill
slots.

The frozen root proposal and review question are:

- `runs/agents/20260821-post-batch008-slate-selection/preliminary-slate.md`;
- `runs/agents/20260821-post-batch008-slate-selection/question.md`.

The preliminary slate contains `RET567`, a proposed proof-producing use of
preexisting child `FP-02.C`, and `EX19`, an information-gathering finite
premise decision under `FP-09`. Both inherit the `decomposable-pascal`
mechanism family, so the concentration exception itself requires review.

## Outcome contract

- `POST-B008-SLATE-SELECTED`: at least one card is dispatch-ready and selected
  with exact classification and correlation accounting.
- `POST-B008-SLATE-EMPTY`: neither card nor any alternative is executable;
  record the exact missing readiness facts without padding.
- `POST-B008-SLATE-REVIEW-GAP`: a precise contract or evidence dispute blocks
  safe selection; freeze the smaller audit and stop.
- `POST-B008-SLATE-PROVIDER-STOP`: preserve the root preliminary decision as a
  proposal if the required bounded strategic review cannot complete.

## Resolution

`POST-B008-SLATE-SELECTED`.

Select **`EX19` only** under `FP-09`. It is an information-gathering exact
decision of the complete abstract Fin19 `9+10` and `10+9` extreme-exchange
premises. The final slate therefore has **zero proof-producing and one
information-gathering slot** and must be described as exploration. Four is a
capacity target rather than a quota; the complete registry audit found no
executable item in another mechanism family and no advancing item to defer.

Detailed planning must treat the two orientations separately unless a
compiling reflection equivalence is available, require independent semantic
reconstruction for SAT and independent formula reconstruction plus proof
replay for UNSAT, forecast formula size and resources, and add an explicit
post-UNSAT stop. A finite UNSAT result does not authorize Lean certificate
promotion, more seeds, certificate engineering, or a `k=11` continuation.

Do **not** select `RET567`. The reciprocal review and root audit correct the
preliminary dependency direction: the current `TwoCapFreeSidesBound567` and
packed balanced-grid branch retain `OneChangeOn` but not decomposability.
`upper_card_le_twenty_one_of_twoCapFreeSides_open_567` consumes that bound; it
does not provide `IsDecomposableOn` inside the proof of the bound. The frozen
card has therefore reached `RET567-SPECIFICATION-STOP` before execution.

A decomposable-specific replacement for the shared two-side leaf remains a
plausible route-amendment candidate, but it would change the mathematical
interface and must receive a separately frozen, reviewed selection decision.
The amendment alone would restore discarded context and is neutral. It is not
part of the selected slate.
