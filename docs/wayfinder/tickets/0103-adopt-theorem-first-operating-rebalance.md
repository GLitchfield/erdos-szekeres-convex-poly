# Ticket 0103: adopt the theorem-first operating rebalance

## Question

After Ticket 0102 closed two approved pre-admission probes at operational
stops, should the project reduce proposal and packet throughput, require a
stronger mathematical entry gate, and rebalance discretionary research toward
the two load-bearing exact-upper routes?

## Decision criteria

1. Put most discretionary mathematical effort on the two load-bearing
   exact-upper routes without treating percentages as scheduling quotas.
2. Require a theorem-first producer/consumer account before proof builders or
   certificate engineering begin.
3. Catch fixture-role and serialization defects before immutable packet freeze
   through independent semantic reconstruction.
4. Preserve smaller honest slates and allow an empty reserve when no
   theorem-ready gate exists.
5. Keep adjacent lemmas demand-driven and do not revive suspended mechanisms
   or stopped services through an operating-policy shortcut.
6. Change no mathematical evidence, portfolio state, or load-bearing ledger
   edge merely by adopting the policy.

## Evidence

The user explicitly approved the operating changes on 2026-08-30 and directed
the project to encode them in the ruleset. This ticket is the durable decision
record. It authorizes documentation and protocol changes only; it does not
approve a mathematical slate, provider call, service restart, task-packet
execution, resource increase, or route-state change.

## Resolution

`THEOREM-FIRST-OPERATING-REBALANCE-ADOPTED`.

Until a later explicit user-approved ticket supersedes this policy, review it
after two complete post-Ticket-0103 frontier cycles or immediately after any
load-bearing ledger edge changes. The review boundary does not automatically
expire the policy.

### 1. Discretionary research allocation

Use the following rolling planning weights for discretionary mathematical
research time. Mandatory reconciliation, independent evidence admission,
guarded builds, repository tests, and integration are outside this percentage
budget and remain required even when they consume more time.

| Share | Focus | Required character |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 40% | `D-HALF` structural work | Prefer a uniform geometric producer for a coherent half-sized predecessor-free support. Use at most one simultaneous Fin15 lane, and only from an independently motivated source for the missing cross-color or selection law. |
| 30% | `D-DECOMP` repair | Seek a boundary-aware invariant or direct injection that repairs the uniform decomposable induction. Do not substitute proof-trace mining, certificate shrinking, or another clipped child summary already ruled out by recorded collisions. |
| 20% | Source-grounded mechanism discovery | Study minimal indecomposable structure or another mathematically distinct exact-upper mechanism with a short consumer ladder and a cheap hostile test. |
| 10% | Bounded computation and process work | Use only decision-changing falsifiers, semantic verification, and necessary integration. Do not use process throughput or larger finite searches as a substitute for a producer theorem. |

`D-NORMALIZE` receives no ordinary execution allocation while its current
mutation-descent family is suspended. Revival still requires the positive
source-side producer or genuinely new monotone mechanism stated in `FP-04`.
Demand-driven adjacent lemmas remain welcome only when an existing named
consumer is already present and the short consumer-ladder rule is satisfied.

### 2. Theorem-entry memo before execution design

Before freezing a proof-producing theorem packet or an intermediary-lemma
packet, record a human-readable theorem-entry memo containing all five fields:

1. the exact producer theorem, including model tier and hypotheses;
2. the existing named downstream consumer;
3. the strict difficulty separation or predeclared measure decrease showing
   why the producer is easier than the open parent obligation;
4. the smallest plausible counterexample or hostile neighboring world; and
5. the geometric, combinatorial, or source-authenticated fact expected to
   produce the theorem.

If any field is missing, the item remains route design. It may receive at most
one separately approved cheap falsifier when that test can change allocation;
it may not receive a Lean builder, certificate-engineering packet, or
proof-producing label.

### 3. Semantic readiness before immutable packet freeze

Every computation or solver packet whose conclusion depends on a finite
fixture, corpus, role label, or serialized mathematical object must have a
separate semantic-readiness receipt before the execution packet is frozen.
The receipt must:

- reconstruct the mathematical object from the raw frozen source rather than
  trust recorded role metadata;
- independently recompute every role-critical predicate used for gate
  precedence, including general position, target-free or strong-free status,
  model membership, and advertised positive or negative control behavior;
- normalize and compare representation boundaries such as tuple/list, index,
  order, sign, and label conventions; and
- stop before outcome-bearing work if any role, canonical representation, or
  independent reconstruction disagrees.

Hash, schema, and provenance validation remain necessary but are not semantic
readiness. The readiness implementation must be independent of the primary
outcome engine at the point where a shared defect could invalidate the role.
An input-readiness failure creates no repair, substitution, retry, or successor
authority.

### 4. Honest slate and reserve policy

Four remains a capacity ceiling and an occupancy target only when four honest
mathematical gates exist. While the reconciled portfolio has no `advancing`
entry and no theorem-ready producer survives the theorem-entry memo, the
default next block is a smaller route-design or source-grounding block, not an
exploration slate padded with campaign support.

Ticket 0103 suspends the default four-packet operational reserve floor in that
state. The honest approved reserve may contain zero, one, two, or three packets;
do not author support work merely to restore a numeric floor. An exploration-
only slate still requires an explicit future user approval and decision-changing
gates. Seed generation and pre-admission development are not automatic spare-
capacity backfill.

### 5. Current route-specific stops

- Do not spend another Fin15 lane merely increasing solver time, proof-file
  allowance, seeds, cube depth, or certificate compression. A Fin15 successor
  must change the mathematical information and name the compiling theorem it
  can close.
- Do not mine accepted state-567 proof traces for another instance-wide
  predicate. A `D-DECOMP` successor must begin with a natural boundary-aware
  statement having independently smaller producer and consumer obligations.
- Do not start a broad intermediary-lemma campaign. Use the existing extension,
  realizability, transport, and transition APIs when a demand-driven consumer
  identifies the missing lemma.

### 6. Service hold

The scheduler remains intentionally paused/stopped, and the continuous idea
generator remains failed/stopped. Neither service may be repaired,
reconfigured, reauthorized, or restarted merely to occupy spare capacity. A
future restart requires a fresh diagnosis and report, an identified theorem-
first workload that passes this ticket, a valid current implementation and
receipt, and a new explicit user decision. Historical receipts remain
replayable records but grant no current execution authority.

## Proof-distance accounting

- **Net proof movement:** neutral.
- **Evidence:** explicit user-approved operating decision plus the reconciled
  Ticket 0102 operational terminals and current portfolio/status checks.
- **Damage scope:** `operational-only`; no mathematical mechanism is damaged or
  promoted by a planning-policy change.
- **Difficulty displaced:** none directly. The uniform `D-HALF` producer,
  uniform `D-DECOMP` repair, `D-NORMALIZE`, and `D-UPPER` remain unchanged.
- **Counterfactual test:** applying these rules earlier would have prevented
  packet and proposal overhead and forced fixture-role checks sooner, but would
  not itself prove a theorem or reduce the mathematical obligation.
- **Planning consequence:** apply the allocation and entry gates before the
  next slate selection; keep both services stopped; prefer a smaller honest
  theorem-design wave when no proof-producing packet qualifies.
- **Load-bearing ledger edge changed:** none.

## Validation contract

The ruleset change is complete only after the Wayfinder, campaign,
orchestration, and portfolio validators pass and the guarded `make test` path
passes. No Lean statement or mathematical route state changes in this ticket.
