# Ticket 0078: adopt the evidence-first research campaign

## Question

Should the one-turn, publication-card-first Research Factory be superseded by
an evidence-first campaign in which compact seeds are attempted immediately,
workers receive selective context, mathematical failures feed scoped mutations,
and claim validation receives capacity before route admission and Lean landing?

## User decision

On 2026-08-24 the user approved all nine process recommendations from the
primary-source review of recent OpenAI, Anthropic, Fable, and comparator
mathematics workflows:

1. full coordinator memory with selective worker context;
2. five brief seeds rather than five pre-audited publication cards;
3. immediate bounded mathematical attempts;
4. evidence-driven mutation of admitted failure residue;
5. scoped serendipitous-result capture (implemented after audit as a sidecar
   to the ordinary primary terminal so it cannot replace the frozen target);
6. a claim-triggered verification funnel;
7. gap-shaped timing for Lean;
8. persistence-neutral prompting; and
9. evidence-production rather than idea-count metrics.

This approval authorizes process documentation, local schemas/checkers and a
bounded pilot design. It does not authorize a provider call, a new
mathematical route, execution of an unapproved mathematical packet, external
mutation, destructive action, commit, or push.

## Decision boundary

The approved change supersedes the discovery semantics of the frozen
`FACTORY-REFRESH-014-CURRENT-STATE-V3` packet. Preserve that packet and its
hashes as a historical control; do not dispatch it unchanged. The rolling
pipeline, root-only evidence admission, immutable task packets, model-tier
firewalls, failure-genome scope discipline, no-padding rule, and canonical
Wayfinder separation remain in force.

This is a research-system change, not a mathematical route selection or
redirection. No bounded Sol/Fable route review is required for the mechanical
implementation of the user-approved process. Any later seed proposed for the
frontier still requires the ordinary route decision and, where applicable,
the bounded strategic review.

## Decision criteria

Resolve this ticket only when all of the following are true:

- one canonical campaign protocol specifies the seed, development, mutation,
  serendipity, verification, Lean-timing, and metric contracts;
- `AGENTS.md` and `docs/agents/README.md` point to that protocol and state
  exactly which older factory rules it supersedes;
- the relevant research profiles no longer require publication-card detail or
  forbid the tools needed for an approved development brief;
- a deterministic local checker validates the new campaign brief and seed
  formats, including selective-context and persistence-neutral safeguards;
- the old Factory v3 packet remains unchanged and is explicitly ineligible for
  dispatch; any prospective control uses a new separately approved wrapper,
  identity, and isolated outputs; and
- repository workflow tests for the changed process artifacts pass.

## Predeclared outcomes

- `CAMPAIGN-PROTOCOL-ADOPTED`: every implementation gate passes; the next
  activity is a separately frozen pilot, not an automatic Sol launch.
- `CAMPAIGN-PROTOCOL-REVISE`: the architecture is coherent but an independent
  audit finds an ambiguous authority, evidence, context, or terminal boundary.
- `CAMPAIGN-PROTOCOL-MISMATCH`: the implementation weakens proof-distance,
  route-admission, model-tier, or failure-scope discipline; retain the old
  protocol until a corrected design is approved.
- `CAMPAIGN-PROTOCOL-STOP`: unrelated worktree conflict or local validation
  prevents a safe isolated change; preserve the approved decision without
  claiming implementation.
