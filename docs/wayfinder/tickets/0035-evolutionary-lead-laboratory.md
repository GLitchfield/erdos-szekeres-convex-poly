# Ticket 0035: prospectively test an evolutionary mathematical-lead laboratory

## Question

Can a bounded evolutionary search over literal mathematical questions produce
more reproducible lead events per unit effort than a matched stratified-random
control, while preserving mechanism diversity and never pretending to know
which questions are good before investigating them?

This is not another semantic-admission classifier and does not reopen
`FP-14.C` at admission.  It is a prospective revival experiment under the
broader `FP-14` research-system scope.  Research Factory v1 and v2a remain
terminal negative knowledge.  Only an observed positive mechanism in this
pilot can justify a later portfolio revival proposal.

## Design principles

- A question is only hard-filtered for an objective defect: malformed or
  ambiguous statement, exact completed duplicate, applicable exact
  counterexample, literal target smuggling, free variable/type mismatch, or no
  bounded first probe.
- An unproved producer is a legitimate research target, not a rejection.
- No gold good/bad labels, semantic oracle, scalar goodness score, or
  retrospective relabeling are used.
- Question quality is not inferred before inquiry.  Resources are allocated
  from observed probe outcomes, diversity, cost, and uncertainty.
- All proof-movement claims remain governed by the repository's compiling
  theorem gate.  Lead events are search information unless a later separate
  Lean packet produces a theorem.

## Genome, phenotype, and memory

The immutable lead genome records parent IDs, target dependency, mechanism
ancestry, island, ordered operator trace, operator parameters, deterministic
seed, and a bounded probe recipe.  Its phenotype is the literal statement and
first experiment.  Surface rewording without a genome or structural change is
not a new lead.

The append-only event ledger records every generated lead, hard-filter result,
probe, evidence artifact, cost, family correlation, deep-investigation
decision, later descendant, and delayed proof or retirement outcome.  Records
are never deleted.  Operator credit is context-specific and may be updated
only by appending a new event linked to the original lineage.

## Islands and operators

The pilot has four islands:

1. proof-graph composition near `D-HALF`, `D-DECOMP`, and `D-NORMALIZE`;
2. geometric, combinatorial, and representation changes;
3. transferred machinery with explicit model/return bridges; and
4. high-variance or deliberately strange mechanisms.

Versioned operators are:

- premise deletion or weakening;
- conclusion weakening or strengthening;
- parameter lifting or restriction;
- dualization or model transfer;
- invariant replacement;
- adjacent consumer--producer composition;
- obstruction-guided mutation;
- counterexample inversion;
- abstraction or concretization;
- crossover through a shared consumer;
- crossover through a shared obstruction; and
- extraction of a failed proof step as a standalone question.

The first generation uses neutral operator priors.  Later selection may update
operator sampling only from recorded outcomes in this run.

## Population and control

Generation zero begins from 32 independently anchored seeds, eight per island.
Within every island, matched seed-family pairs are assigned by a frozen hash
coin to the evolutionary and control arms, four seeds per island per arm.
Each arm produces exactly 160 offspring using the same neutral operator
distribution.  After objective hard filters and correlation clustering,
32 candidates per arm receive matched stratified-random probes.  Generation
zero therefore measures the arm baseline before adaptive selection.

Generation one produces exactly 160 new offspring:

- 80 from Pareto/novelty evolutionary breeding; and
- 80 from uniform control parents and operators.

Another 64 receive matched probes, 32 per arm.  Thus each arm receives exactly
64 allocated probes over the pilot.  Evolutionary generation-one parent
selection uses its own generation-zero outcomes, a Pareto frontier, novelty
archive, and 30 percent unconditional exploration.  Control parents and
operators are fixed by a pre-outcome uniform seed and cannot depend on any
probe result.  Arm ancestry is closed after the initial paired assignment;
there is no cross-arm crossover or migration.  At most 25 percent of allocated
probes may come from one mechanism family and at most 25 percent may be
attributed to one operator.  No scalar fitness is used.

At most eight leads receive 20--40 minute deep investigations, and at most two
may belong to one mechanism family.  Exactly four are selected from each arm
when both arms have four eligible leads; otherwise the unfilled slots remain
empty.  Deep results are excluded from the primary arm ratio.  No Lean
formalization, solver scale-up, or external literature search is part of this
ticket.

## Evidence vector

Each completed probe receives a vector, not an overall grade:

- evidence strength;
- information gained;
- downstream specificity;
- reusable structure;
- novelty relative to tested lineages;
- tractability and active cost; and
- correlation/crowding.

A reproducible lead event must cite a named consumer or destination dependency,
carry the frozen outcome-specific certificate, and pass a separately authored
replay validator.  It is one of:

- a deterministic positive model or counterexample independently replayed;
- an exact source/consumer composition newly exposed by authenticated project
  interfaces;
- an exact formalizable theorem or falsifier contract with premise provenance,
  named consumer, and a bounded discriminating test accepted by independent
  review; or
- an evidence-backed mechanism retirement.

At most two events per mechanism family per arm count toward the endpoint.
Positive models or counterexamples that do not toggle a named premise,
conclusion, consumer, or registered mechanism decision do not count.  Deep
results and delayed descendants do not change the primary count; delayed
credit closes at the seven-hour stop.  These events remain proposal or
checked-computation evidence according to their actual gate.  They are not
Lean theorems.

The single primary endpoint is the count of replay-valid lead events per arm
among exactly 64 allocated micro-probes.  Evidence vectors and full
arm-attributable effort, including generation, filtering, probing, replay, and
review, are descriptive only.  No post-outcome weighted score or per-minute
metric may select the terminal.

## Decision criteria

Proceed only with the frozen prospective comparison: 32 independently
anchored seeds, neutral generation-zero operators in both sealed arms, 64
allocated micro-probes per arm over two generations, arm-blind execution and
review, replayable event certificates, and the diversity and ancestry checks
stated below.  Do not use old question-card labels as training data or treat a
missing compiling producer as grounds to reject an otherwise literal research
question.

At the seven-hour boundary, apply the terminal precedence and numeric outcome
thresholds below without backfill, threshold tuning, retrospective relabeling,
or delayed credit.  A positive comparison changes only the research-process
portfolio: it permits a separately reviewed revival proposal for an adaptive
lead generator.  It does not promote a mathematical route or alter any
load-bearing destination dependency.  A baseline signal retains the event and
operator memory but disables fitness-weighted breeding; no signal or collapse
ends this pilot family absent a genuinely new positive mechanism.

## Outcome contract

- `ELL-REVIVAL-REJECT`: Sol/Fable finds that this is merely another correlated
  classifier refinement, has a gameable yield definition, or lacks a fair
  prospective control.  Stop before generation.
- `ELL-EVOLUTION-SIGNAL`: at least eight reproducible lead events total, at
  least two independently reviewed deep next gates, all diversity caps hold,
  and the evolutionary arm has at least 1.25 times the control arm's event
  count.  If the control has zero events, the evolutionary arm must supply all
  eight required total events.
- `ELL-BASELINE-SIGNAL`: the total-event and deep-gate conditions hold but the
  evolutionary arm does not meet the advantage condition.  Retain the event
  ledger and useful operators, disable fitness-weighted breeding, and do not
  claim a genetic-search advantage.
- `ELL-NO-SIGNAL`: fewer than eight reproducible events or fewer than two deep
  next gates.  Preserve the ledger and do not schedule another refinement.
- `ELL-COLLAPSE`: family/operator concentration, duplicate phenotype growth,
  ancestry leakage between arms, or post-outcome selection invalidates the
  comparison.
- `ELL-RESOURCE-STOP`: a hard resource or wall-time cap prevents terminal
  scoring.

Every outcome has neutral proof movement.  A positive outcome can only support
a separately reviewed process-revival proposal.  It cannot promote a
mathematical route or alter `D-UPPER`, `D-HALF`, `D-DECOMP`, or
`D-NORMALIZE`.

Terminal precedence is deterministic: review rejection; resource failure;
integrity collapse; insufficient total events or deep gates; baseline signal;
then evolutionary signal.  Collapse fires on any cross-arm ancestry, any
unblinded probe or event review, any post-outcome backfill, family/operator
probe concentration above 25 percent, or duplicate structural fingerprints
above 10 percent of either arm's eligible population.

All operator application, hard filtering, probing, event replay, and event
review receive anonymized arm-free records.  Only the root selector sees arm
assignments.  Hard filters are reject-side only: a bounded normalized check may
reject an exact duplicate, applicable recorded counterexample, target
occurrence, stored witness flow, malformed/free-variable statement, or
model-type mismatch.  Every rejection needs a replayable certificate.  Hitting
a normalization or graph cap keeps the lead eligible and records an unresolved
hygiene check; it never rejects the lead.

## Execution slate

This is exploration with zero proof-producing and three
information-gathering assignments:

1. evolutionary operator, lineage, and deterministic population construction;
2. near-route proof-graph and obstruction micro-probes; and
3. representation-transfer, high-variance, and independent evidence audit.

The mechanism-family concentration is explicit: the experimental object is one
search instrument and its matched control, not three purportedly independent
proof routes.  Root owns task freezes, random-control sampling, execution
manifest, arm opening, scoring, and all Wayfinder edits.

## Resources and approvals

Forecast is 180--360 wall minutes with a hard seven-hour stop.  Each local
process is capped at 30 minutes; aggregate resources are eight CPU cores,
16 GiB RAM, and 2 GiB run artifacts.  The approved block authorizes one exact
Sol/Fable provider review, three specialist assignments, local bounded Python
execution, ignored run artifacts, this ticket, timing records, and final
portfolio reconciliation.

It does not authorize Lean/Lake, solver scale-up, literature/network access
beyond the provider review, software installation, destructive action, commit,
push, or external repository mutation.  Any lead that warrants those actions
receives a later separately approved packet.

## Resolution

Closed 2026-08-18 as `ELL-RESOURCE-STOP`.

The prospective semantic generator, corrected Pareto-plus-novelty planner, and
balanced hidden-arm selector passed their frozen infrastructure gates.  The
mathematical comparison did not.  Both investigators independently found all
64 selected statements semantically ungrounded before computation.  The
validated terminal ledgers contain 51 authenticated-source `no_signal` rows
and 13 non-counted operational-stop rows, with zero grounded probes, candidate
events, independent replays, or counted events.  The near-route wrapper hit
its 600-second cap after 19 attempts, and the frozen scorer separately failed
a synthetic fixture before it could be released.  Generation one, deep gates,
and arm scoring were not run.  Terminal precedence therefore forbids both an
arm-comparison claim and an `ELL-NO-SIGNAL` claim.

**Net proof movement:** neutral.  **Evidence:** authenticated-source null
records, checked research-process infrastructure, and operational stops; no
compiling theorem or checked mathematical computation.  **Difficulty
displaced:** none; every advertised probe remains upstream of an undefined
predicate, producer, or model bridge.  **Counterfactual:** this result avoids
computing on statements without semantics and improves a later experimental
design, but it does not reduce exact-conjecture proof work.  **Planning
consequence:** expire ELL v0.1, promote no generated lead or descendant, and
keep `FP-14.C` suspended.  No load-bearing edge changes; `D-UPPER`, `D-HALF`,
`D-DECOMP`, and `D-NORMALIZE` are unchanged.

Revival requires a genuinely new positive mechanism: an independently defined
executable primitive vocabulary or interpreter, a prospective
groundability-only check, bounded batched interface checking, a fully passing
pre-assignment scorer, and a small arm-balanced canary containing actually
grounded probes.  Repairing thresholds or rerunning these lineages is not a
revival condition.
