# Ticket 0047: run a mixed four-thread grounding slate

## Question

Can one bounded proof-engineering gate and three deliberately exploratory
mechanism probes produce either a kernel-checked Fin11 leaf or sufficiently
sharp search information to decide whether three factory proposals deserve a
later portfolio-admission review?

## Decision criteria

Execute the four frozen gates exactly once and classify each outcome against
the predeclared branches below.  Only a compiling theorem that strictly closes
a named dependency counts as proof movement; finite experiments and operational
stops may change resource allocation but cannot promote a route by themselves.

## Approved slate

The user approved this four-thread slate on 2026-08-20.  Four is being used as
the available concurrency target, not as evidence that the four mechanisms are
equally mature.

1. **Fin11 balanced/checkpointed LRAT replay** (`proof-producing`, `FP-14.B`).
   Preserve the exact selected depth-six cube, reconstructed 9,181-clause core,
   18,349 LRAT additions, and standard Lean/LRAT trust boundary.  Replace only
   the failed monolithic proof-expression shape by dependency-aware balanced
   checkpoints.  Run at most one guarded Lean process for 1,800 seconds and
   12,288 MiB.  Success is a compiling theorem for this fixed leaf only; it
   does not establish the uniform predecessor producer.
2. **Projection-order predecessor coloring** (`information-gathering`,
   predecessor-half family).  On frozen realizable controls, enumerate generic
   Euclidean projection-order cells and test whether Pluhar's ordered two-chain
   obstruction is absent for at least one cell.  Use two independent exact
   implementations, at most two cores, 2 GiB, twenty minutes, and 256 MiB of
   output.  Finite survival cannot revive `FP-07` without a uniform geometric
   producer theorem and a bridge to the existing recurrence.
3. **Antipodal Monge endpoint ranks** (`information-gathering`, ambient
   endpoint-rank family).  Compute full cap/cup endpoint-rank arrays on frozen
   realizable controls and test one global opposite-sign Monge law.  Only if
   that producer survives may a separately prefrozen abstract-array stage test
   `MongeEndpointMeet`.  Each stage is capped at one core, 1 GiB, ten minutes.
   A finite pass does not reduce `D-HALF` without both a uniform law and the
   meeting theorem.
4. **Deep-split tangle/tree defects** (`information-gathering`, normalization
   family).  Enumerate ternary context-bearing separations through eight
   vertices, test the frozen numerical submodularity law with independent
   implementations, and invoke a prospectively frozen structural fallback
   only when the numerical law survives.  Cap at one core, 2 GiB, twenty
   minutes, and 512 MiB of output.  A finite dichotomy does not revive `FP-04`
   without a geometric theorem excluding the tangle branch.

All delegated writes are confined to disjoint ignored run directories.  The
orchestrator owns this ticket, shared documentation, all builders, integration,
and the post-run factory packet.  The heavy Fin11 Lean gate runs only after the
three computational lanes are terminal, preventing aggregate-memory
contention.  Producer/checker sources and outcome commands are frozen before
execution; paired outputs remain sealed until both processes terminate; there
is no post-outcome repair or rerun.

## Outcome contract

### Fin11

- `FIN11-CHECKPOINT-LAND`: the unchanged fixed-leaf theorem passes direct Lean,
  the targeted gate, and a declaration-only standard-axiom audit below the
  frozen resource cap.
- `FIN11-CHECKPOINT-RESOURCE`: the frozen gate reaches its time or memory cap.
- `FIN11-CHECKPOINT-MODEL-GAP`: the exact LRAT dependency structure cannot be
  decomposed into independently kernel-checkable checkpoints without changing
  the theorem or trust boundary.
- `FIN11-CHECKPOINT-TRUST-STOP`: the only discovered implementation would
  weaken kernel/LRAT trust or use a forbidden proof shortcut.

### Projection order

- `PROJECTION-TWOCHAIN-FALSIFIED`: every projection cell of an admitted control
  contains an ordered two-chain; retain the smallest exact witness.
- `PROJECTION-TWOCHAIN-SURVIVES`: at least one cell avoids the obstruction on
  every admitted control, with independent agreement.
- `PROJECTION-TWOCHAIN-MODEL-GAP`: required realizations, projection cells, or
  predecessor semantics cannot be authenticated exactly.
- `PROJECTION-TWOCHAIN-OPERATIONAL`: the frozen checker pair disagrees or a cap
  prevents classification.

### Antipodal Monge

- `MONGE-PRODUCER-FALSIFIED`: an admitted realizable control violates the
  global opposite-sign rank law.
- `MONGE-ENDPOINT-MEET-FALSIFIED`: Stage 1 survives but a prefrozen abstract
  rank array refutes the proposed meeting implication.
- `MONGE-FINITE-SURVIVES`: both authorized finite stages survive independently.
- `MONGE-MODEL-GAP`: exact endpoint-rank or recurrence semantics cannot be
  frozen without inventing a bridge.
- `MONGE-CHECKER-MISMATCH`: independent computations disagree or a cap fires.

### Deep split

- `TANGLE-NUMERICAL-PASS`: the frozen numerical submodularity law survives the
  admitted finite systems.
- `TANGLE-STRUCTURAL-ONLY`: numerical submodularity fails but the prospectively
  frozen structural fallback still yields the declared finite dichotomy.
- `TANGLE-FALSIFIED`: both declared specializations fail, with the smallest
  exact witness retained.
- `TANGLE-MODEL-GAP`: the proposed separation order or forbidden stars cannot
  be defined from authenticated source semantics.
- `TANGLE-OPERATIONAL`: independent enumerators disagree or a cap fires.

## Integration and continuation

After all four terminalize, admit each evidence gate independently, update the
map, affected portfolio entries, negative knowledge, idea ledger, timing data,
and this ticket, and run the slate/portfolio validators.  No finite survivor is
automatically promoted.  Then rebuild the deterministic whole-project Sol
packet from the reconciled state and request exactly five semantically new
ideas from distinct mechanism families, with prior ideas and negative results
included for duplicate avoidance.

## Resolution

The four-thread slate terminalized on 2026-08-20 with all task/result and slate
contracts validated.

- **Fin11:** `FIN11-CHECKPOINT-LAND`. The unchanged 9,181-clause CNF and
  18,349-addition LRAT leaf compile as 32 kernel-checked checkpoints under the
  exact public declaration
  `synchronizedFiveFanFin11_indexedCubeLeaf_lrat_unsat`. The serial targeted
  builds plus direct final-file gate passed in 298.2 seconds. One Lean process
  was maintained, and the highest returned live RSS sample was 1,956.0 MiB.
  The declaration audit reports only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and
  `Quot.sound`.
- **Projection order:** `PROJECTION-TWOCHAIN-OPERATIONAL`. Exact-coordinate
  controls and two candidate evaluators were prepared, but the immutable
  twenty-minute lane budget expired before source freeze or outcome execution.
  The proposal remains mathematically untested.
- **Antipodal Monge:** `MONGE-PRODUCER-FALSIFIED`. Two prospectively frozen
  independent implementations agree across 374 endpoint-rank ledgers. The
  smallest admitted witness is the recursive-lower eight-point control, whose
  rectangles realize incompatible deltas `(-1,+1)` and `(0,-1)`. The exact
  one-global-sign producer is expired, and Stage 2 was correctly not run.
- **Deep split:** `TANGLE-OPERATIONAL`. The frozen producer raised `KeyError`
  before controls or enumeration because a `dict.get` default was evaluated
  eagerly. No checker or structural fallback ran, so the mathematical card is
  wholly untested.

The reconciled whole-project packet then drove Sol Batch 006. It authenticated
7,568 project artifacts, the 1,128-entry legacy registry, and all twenty-five
earlier Sol proposals before packaging exactly five proposals in five new
mechanism families. Those proposals are search information only and were added
to the idea ledger with `uninvestigated` status; no portfolio admission or
execution selection occurred.

**Net proof movement:** forward only on the predeclared fixed Fin11 leaf
obligation; the other three lanes are neutral. **Evidence:** one compiling
project theorem, one checked finite falsifier, and two operational stops.
**Difficulty displaced:** the selected leaf's 14.9-GiB monolithic kernel
barrier is removed rather than moved into a premise. The indexed semantic
producer, other leaves, complete Fin11 theorem, uniform predecessor producer,
and deep-tangle exclusion remain. **Counterfactual:** knowing these outcomes
would eliminate the exact Monge card and the failed operational runs and would
replace the selected-leaf replay risk by compiled infrastructure, but it would
not change the estimated work on `D-HALF` or `D-NORMALIZE`. **Planning
consequence:** retain the checkpoint architecture; do not expand to all cubes
until a named semantic consumer is frozen; expire the literal Monge producer;
leave projection and tangle untested rather than auto-rerunning them. No
load-bearing destination edge changed. Route-review Batch 006 separately
before scheduling any of its proposals.
