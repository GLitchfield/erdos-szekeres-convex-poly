## Lane FAN13: compatible size-six singleton-fan extension screen

### Objective and model-tier firewall

Test a proposed one-vertex extension mechanism at the first ambient size
needed after the compiled Fin11 size-six singleton fan.  This is **not** the
first possible counterexample to the unrestricted statement, because the
general inequality `2 * side.card < n` permits smaller sides.  It is the first
`n=13` screen for extending the size-six starting instance from cardinality
six to seven.

The abstract tier is a `TripleColoring 13` satisfying `OneChangeOn` and no
strong six-set.  The geometric tier additionally requires an exact
fixed-sweep integer realization of every triple sign.  Abstract SAT rejects
only the abstract producer.  A sweep-realizable rejection requires exact
coordinates.  Finite UNSAT revives neither suspended `FP-01` nor completed
finite child `FP-01.A`.

### Frozen mathematical card

Use two directions, `lowerCup` and `upperCap`.  Before encoding, F0 must copy
the exact project predicates and freeze:

- `PureSingletonFan chi direction side pivot`: exactly the corresponding
  directional branch of the compiled Fin11 synchronized-fan conclusion,
  omitting only its fixed-cardinality wrapper;
- `InsertCompatible chi direction side pivot x`: exactly the new
  pivot-crossing triple requirements and every new strong-five purity
  requirement introduced by adding `x`; and
- the proposed implication that every size-six pure fan in a premise-valid
  Fin13 coloring has an outside compatible vertex.

F0 must prove or finitely verify, without using the desired global selector:

1. the exact ordering and nonvacuity semantics of `HasDeepBelowSplit`;
2. restriction/transport from the compiled Fin11 theorem gives every
   premise-valid Fin13 coloring at least one size-six pure fan, or the frozen
   formula must carry an explicit equivalent fan-existence condition;
3. `x` outside `side ∪ {pivot}` gives seven distinct side vertices after
   insertion;
4. insertion preserves every deep-split, crossing-triple, and new strong-five
   purity field required by the proposed next fan; and
5. for `n=13`, the natural deficit `((n + 1) / 2) - side.card` falls from one
   at side size six to zero at side size seven.

Failure, ambiguity, or vacuity in any item stops before CNF generation.

### Frozen countermodel formula and stages

After F0, freeze one formula asserting that there exists a premise-valid

## Lane LONESUM: choice-free cross-middle decomposition specification

### Objective and consumer ladder

The local representation is exact: for each middle vertex `j`, define

```text
M_j(i,k) = 1  iff  chi(i,j,k) = left,
```

on its complete left-by-right domain.  The eight `OneChangeOn` words imply the
local lonesum/checkerboard law.  That fact is lossless and useful search
information, but it is not a global decomposition or a bound.

Test whether one **choice-free cross-middle construction** can support the
consumer ladder

```text
rooted binary decomposition of depth at most k-2 with lossless leaves
  -> at most 2^(k-2) distinct vertices
  -> D-UPPER.
```

The first arrow is the proposed structural theorem.  The counting consequence
must be explicit; a forest with an uncontrolled number of roots, lost
vertices, or noninjective decoding does not enter the ladder.

### L0 specification gate

Before any empirical control, freeze and prove or falsify all of the following
for the proposed construction:

1. a tagged global node type preserving middle index, row/column side,
   original vertex identity, and multiplicity;
2. canonical restriction maps when left/right domains change;
3. explicit empty-quadrant handling and a no-information-loss invariant;
4. a total map from every ambient vertex to a terminal leaf and an injection
   or bijection sufficient for the desired cardinality bound;
5. unique parenthood;
6. exactly one root, or an exact root accounting that still gives the target
   constant without hiding a multiplicative factor;
7. at most two children for every internal node;
8. an exact strict-split depth convention and a proof obligation that avoiding
   a strong `k`-set bounds depth by `k-2`;
9. a deterministic decoder selecting distinct vertices, with no
   outcome-dependent orientation, representative, or tie break; and
10. the precise quantifier over **every** middle matrix induced by the chosen
    sorted vertices, followed by a direct `isMiddleDeterminedOn` check.

If any item cannot be stated without a choice whose invariance is itself
unproved, the lane stops at `LONESUM-SPEC-GAP`.  Definition repair after seeing
controls is forbidden.

### Mandatory controls and stages

- `L0 — specification` (90 minutes): freeze the ten items above, the exact
  structural theorem, the counting corollary, and two independent checker
  interfaces.
- `L1 — tightness calibration` (30 minutes): instantiate the exact `k=6`,
  16-point recursive lower extremal construction behind
  `classical_lower_sharpness`.  It must retain all vertices, attain the correct
  depth/root count, and avoid a strong six-set.  Failure to obtain a
  source-faithful executable construction is a specification/control stop.
- `L2 — control battery` (at most 15 compute minutes): apply the frozen
  construction to the first prospectively selected nontrivial recursively
  decomposable control with at least two strict refinements, plus frozen
  abstract `OneChangeOn`, sweep-realizable nondecomposable, empty-quadrant, and
  crossing-domain controls.  Check every parent, root, child, depth,
  information-loss, and decoder obligation independently.
- `L3 — disposition` (15 minutes): retain the first exact failure verbatim or
  emit one surviving card.  Do not repair and rerun.

### Outcomes and downstream plan

- `LONESUM-CARD-ADMIT`: the entire construction and tightness control survive,
  a positive control has multiple strict refinements, and the decoder succeeds.
  This is still proposal evidence; route-review possible admission as a new

- `LONESUM-CROSS-MIDDLE-FALSIFIED`: an authenticated control violates the
