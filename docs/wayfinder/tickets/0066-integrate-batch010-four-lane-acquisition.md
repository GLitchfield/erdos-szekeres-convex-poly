# Ticket 0066: integrate Batch 010 four-lane acquisition

## Question

After grounding the four non-predecessor candidates authorized by Ticket 0065,
which outcomes are admissible into the canonical frontier, and does the
resulting pool satisfy the user's hard four-lane prerequisite for any research
execution?

## Decision criteria

Admit delegated work only after its task/result gate, exact computation or
symbolic derivation, and independent root check pass. Apply the narrowest
damage scope, require one bounded discriminating gate and a lawful consumer
ladder for portfolio admission, and do not count a correct but inert theorem,
an expired candidate, an operational stop, or an unreviewed backfill as a
lane. A valid four-card pool may contain at most two `predecessor-half` cards.

This ticket integrates and reconciles the acquisition block. It does not run
WIN11, LCOUNT, BETA2's transforms or cones, another factory batch, or any
research execution slate.

## Resolution

Outcome: `FOUR-LANE-NOT-READY`.

Only corrected BETA2 passes admission. It becomes foundational probing entry
`FP-19`, family `hereditary-beta-cardinality-cones`, with an information-
gathering gate over three frozen transforms followed by exact rational cones
at `(9,5)` and `(17,6)`. Those computations have not run.

The other grounded candidates do not count:

1. **B010-I01 / COPOINT — expire candidate-locally.** The authenticated
   realizable six-point control has the required unique nine-antihole, but all
   six membership signatures are distinct and its attachment set contains no
   strong five- or six-set. The frozen target-or-fiber-split dichotomy is
   false. This does not damage Morris's theorem, SPGT, other copoint
   statistics, or the decomposable architecture.
2. **B010-I02 / HEAP — ledger-only operational stop.** The Elnitsky source
   authenticated, but the 75-minute wall fired before any soundness,
   invariance, consumer-gap, or counterexample evidence gate.
3. **B010-I03 / LAMBDA — gated ledger-only.** Exact independent replay
   recovers the recursive root cuts with isolation indices 5 and 8, while 53
   of 55 positive cuts are nongeometric and both residual metrics are nonzero
   split-prime. The proposed inequality `d_P(i,j) >= n-2` is true from the two
   diagonal columns but closes no named dependency and discriminates none of
   the live explanations. No replacement gate is invented.

WIN11 (`FP-17`) and LCOUNT (`FP-18`) remain unstale, frozen, and unrun. With
BETA2 (`FP-19`), the honest pool has three cards: one proof-producing and two
information-gathering. WIN11 and LCOUNT are fully correlated in
`predecessor-half`; BETA2 is separate. Since three is less than four, no
execution slate is selected, planned for execution, or dispatched. No
advancing high-leverage entry is deferred because the portfolio contains no
entry in state `advancing`.
