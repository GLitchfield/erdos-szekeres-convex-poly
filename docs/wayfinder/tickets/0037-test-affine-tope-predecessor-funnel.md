# Ticket 0037: test the affine-tope predecessor funnel

## Question

Does every target-free realizable planar point set admit a strict affine line
whose two sides both avoid the predecessor target, and can the first bounded
cycle either falsify that strengthening or expose a genuinely simpler uniform
producer?

The unrestricted integral predecessor-bicoloring and half-subset consumers
already compile.  This ticket does not re-prove them.  It tests a new geometric
producer suggested by the completed rotation/lattice question atlas.

## Evidence admission and reconciliation

The atlas completed one auditable attempt for every question RLQ-001 through
RLQ-254.  Its strongest new search signal is the affine-tope condition:

- all 77 realizable convex-five-free order types on eight points admit a
  strict line-separated 4+4 predecessor split;
- all 88,423 realizable convex-six-free order types on nine points admit a
  strict line-separated 4+5 predecessor split; and
- both claims have independently authored exact replays.

These are complete censuses only at their stated sizes.  They are checked
search information, not a uniform theorem.  The sharp relevant boundary for
the six-point target is sixteen points.

The existing predecessor-half family has a long negative streak.  FP-01
remains suspended: this ticket does not retune the expired single-cell,
adaptive-selector, local-exchange, interval, or maximum-support descendants.
FP-07.A instead restricts the entire bicoloring to a zero-free affine tope.
That is a new mechanism, but its three gates are correlated and count as one

## Candidate hierarchy

For a target-free general-position point set:

1. a two-sided strict affine predecessor cut;
2. one affine half-space containing at least half the points and avoiding the
   predecessor target;
3. the unrestricted `PredecessorHalfSubsetAt` contract.

The implications from 1 to 2 to 3 are not the new mathematics.  The only
load-bearing payoff is a producer for one of these statements.

## Execution slate

information-gathering slots:

1. P1: realizable adversarial search through the sixteen-point boundary;

more finite survival, the child is suspended rather than refined again.

## P1: realizable adversarial search

### Frozen test

A candidate is an exact integer-coordinate, general-position,
convex-six-free labeled point set.  Enumerate every strict linearly separable
nontrivial bipartition.  Record:

- the number whose two sides are both convex-five-free;
- the number with at least one side of cardinality at least half that is
  convex-five-free;
- exact separator certificates; and
- the sorted-x chirotope and generator genealogy.

A zero two-sided count falsifies only the strongest affine-cut child.  A zero
one-sided-half count falsifies the entire affine half-support child, but not
unrestricted predecessor bicoloring or D-HALF.

### Search and controls

Begin from independently checked recursive lower-construction seeds.
Permitted mutations are exact coordinate perturbations, controlled
wall-crossings, crossover of independently verified construction blocks, and
point insertion/deletion followed by a full predicate replay.  Optimize
lexicographically for target-freeness, fewer two-sided cuts, fewer one-sided
half supports, and genealogy-balanced diversity.

Required controls include the N=8/N=9 complete-census certificates, a convex
six-set corruption, a collinearity corruption, an invalid-separator
corruption, a target-containing child-side corruption, and a relabeling that
must preserve the geometric answer.

### Staged coverage

Gate A searches sizes 10 through 13.  Gate B searches sizes 14 through 16 only
if:

- a verified target-free seed exists at every requested size; and
- Gate A reports at least 10,000 distinct exact sorted-x chirotope bitstrings
  across at least four independently described generator genealogies, with no
  genealogy contributing more than one half of the retained corpus.

The 10,000 threshold is a search-quality heuristic, not a completeness claim.
Every survival outcome reports exact per-size and per-genealogy counts.

### Outcomes

- `P1-TWO-SIDED-FALSIFIED`: exact realizable zero-cut witness.
- `P1-AFFINE-HALF-FALSIFIED`: the witness also has no valid one-sided affine
  half support.
- `P1-LOW-MARGIN`: no falsifier, but a witness with one or two two-sided cuts.
- `P1-SAMPLED-SURVIVAL`: no falsifier in the exact frozen sampled search.
- `P1-SEED-GAP`, `P1-CHECKER-MISMATCH`, or `P1-RESOURCE`.

No survival label may contain `boundary`, `uniform`, or `theorem`.

## P2: abstract topes and realizability

### Frozen test

For a fixed acyclic rank-three chirotope, enumerate zero-free covectors/topes
using an explicit oriented-matroid definition and ask whether one tope has both
restrictions predecessor-free.  Arbitrary vertex 2-colorings are not topes.
A failure to derive topes from the frozen abstract object is `P2-MODEL-GAP`,
not license to inspect coordinates.

### Stages

1. reproduce all realizable N=8/N=9 affine-cut answers from chirotope data;
2. run the exact test on the existing complete target-free Fin8 signotope
   corpus; and
3. extend only while the bounded generator and independent checker remain
   within the frozen cap.  Stop before SAT/LRAT certificate engineering.

Any `P2-REALIZABLE-FALSIFIER` must include exact coordinates or another
positive, independently checked realizability certificate.  Failure to prove
nonstretchability is not realizability evidence.

### Outcomes

- `P2-ABSTRACT-FALSIFIER`: a checked abstract failure; realizability becomes
  essential.
- `P2-REALIZABLE-FALSIFIER`: a positively realized failure; this also closes
  P1 on its corresponding scope.
- `P2-SAMPLED-PASS`: finite abstract survival only.
- `P2-MODEL-GAP`, `P2-CHECKER-MISMATCH`, or `P2-RESOURCE`.

## P3: cut-space obstruction and theorem-card forge

The strict affine cuts form the cells of a dual line arrangement.  Each convex
predecessor edge forbids cells where it is monochromatic.  Failure of the
desired producer is exactly coverage of all cells by these forbidden sets.
Because this restatement is target-equivalent, incidence data alone cannot
become a theorem card.

### Work

1. Compute exact forbidden-cell incidence, valid-cell components, blocker
   numbers, and near-cover witnesses for all N=8 types and a frozen stratified
   N=9 sample containing every minimum-cut type.
2. Attempt three source-first mechanisms:
   - a bounded minimal-cover or nerve obstruction;
   - a first/last monochromatic-edge handoff in an allowable sequence; and
   - an antipodal Tucker/Borsuk--Ulam obstruction on cut space.
3. For every proposed card, state exact source and target propositions, an
   independently produced source witness, a strict downstream complexity
   decrease, a premise-occurrence audit, and one deletion corruption for every
   load-bearing source fact.
4. Mechanically erase the conclusion, every successful-cut indicator, and
   every predecessor-free support witness.  The source predicate must remain
   computable and at least one frozen control must change when each claimed
   source fact is deleted.

Primary-source authentication records exact theorem statements and proof
mechanisms.  A citation or analogy alone is `P3-NO-CARD`.

### Outcomes

- `P3-CARD-FROZEN`: one literal noncircular card with a credible producer and
  direct consumer into P1 or P2.  This authorizes discussion only.
- `P3-FALSIFIED`: exact finite counterexample to every frozen card.
- `P3-PRODUCER-GAP`: a card assumes a successful cut or equivalent support.
- `P3-NO-CARD`, `P3-CHECKER-MISMATCH`, or `P3-RESOURCE`.

## Resolution

Approved by the user and completed on 2026-08-19.  The correlated slate ends
`AFFINE-TOPE-FUNNEL-REFUTED`.

P1 found an exact 13-point integer realization in general position with no
convex six-set, no two-sided affine predecessor cut, and no one-sided affine
predecessor-free half support.  Its 78 nontrivial unoriented cuts were replayed
independently.  Gate B was skipped because Gate A was decisive and its staged
coverage threshold was not met.  P2 reconstructed the same witness as a
uniform acyclic rank-three realizable chirotope with 158 oriented topes and the
OneChange law, so realizability is not an escape from the failure.

P3 independently finds that all 156 oriented nontrivial cells are forbidden;
five predecessor edges form a minimum full cover.  The nerve, allowable-
sequence handoff, and Tucker/Borsuk--Ulam cards all fail the frozen source and
deletion audit.  In particular, the natural obstruction label is antipodally
even, whereas the Tucker mechanism requires an odd/equivariant labeling.

**Net proof movement:** neutral.  **Evidence:** independently checked exact
computation and primary-source audit.  **Difficulty displaced:** none;
`D-HALF`, unrestricted integral predecessor Property B, and the fractional
route are unchanged.  **Counterfactual:** the witness prevents further work on
this false affine child, but does not shorten the remaining exact proof.
**Planning consequence:** suspend `FP-07.A`; retain the parent and sibling
routes without promotion.  The three gates count as one negative outcome in
the predecessor-half mechanism family.
