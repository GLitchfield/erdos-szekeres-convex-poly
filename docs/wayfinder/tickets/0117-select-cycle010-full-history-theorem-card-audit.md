## Decision criteria

### Freshness and theorem-entry gate

Every lane must first compare its possible statement against:

1. the complete current parent `FP-NN` record, including every failure genome,
   expiry/revival condition, and retained gate-history paragraph;
2. every ticket and theorem identity cited for the same producer, conclusion,
   or obstruction;
3. alpha-equivalent and premise-patched statements, not only identical names;
4. correlated siblings whose failure cause already applies; and
5. the current load-bearing consumer rather than a historical or closed one.

A lane may return at most one qualified card.  It qualifies only if it gives:

1. a literal Lean-shaped theorem or exact falsifier at an explicit model tier;
2. a named current compiling producer which does not contain the conclusion
   under a synonym;
3. a named current consumer, with at most one speculative arrow after the
   proposed theorem;
4. obligations before and after with a strict decrease in open cases,
   hypothesis strength, quantifier complexity, certificate size, or dependency
   depth; and
5. the cheapest hostile control, bounded evidence gate, outcome branches, and
   stop condition.

The lane must also list its nearest historical matches and explain the exact
new mathematical information.  If no card passes, it must state the bounded
source/API pool audited and return its declared exhaustion terminal.  A true
local identity, conditional interface, rephrased missing premise, restored
lossless state, larger finite instance, or source suggestion without a current
producer and consumer does not qualify.
