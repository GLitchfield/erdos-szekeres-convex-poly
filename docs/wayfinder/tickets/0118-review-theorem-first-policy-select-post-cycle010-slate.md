## Resolution

### Policy review

Retain Ticket 0103 and the `40/30/20/10` rolling planning weights unchanged.

- The theorem-entry gate repeatedly prevented target-restating or
  context-erasing builders; the counterfactuals consistently say those
  rejected builders would not have lowered the proof estimate.
- Cycles 007--010 contain useful search information but no forward theorem or
  load-bearing edge change.  Activity counts therefore do not justify
  reweighting.
- Missing route-specific CPU/RSS instrumentation prevents a calibrated
  comparison of the numerical weights.  Future cycles must enforce the
  already mandatory actual wall-time and resource record; this is protocol
  compliance, not a policy amendment.
- Ticket 0117's stop of the bounded internal static-card loop is now binding
  selection guidance.  It does not suspend either load-bearing mechanism.
- The `30%` `D-DECOMP` share remains coupled to suspended `D-NORMALIZE` before
  it can close `D-UPPER`; examine that coupling at the next instrumented policy
  review, but current evidence does not justify a weight change.

Review the policy after two complete post-Ticket-0118 frontier cycles, or
immediately after a load-bearing ledger edge change or literal route revival.
Do not manufacture cycles to reach the boundary.
