existing stable mechanism family, admitted as a new frontier entry, retained

## Decision criteria

Apply the complete portfolio-admission, skeptical proof-distance,
exploratory-patience, model-tier, and architecture-correlation rules in
`AGENTS.md`. A proposal needs a concrete observation or source-backed mechanism

## Resolution

Outcome: `BATCH009-LEDGER-ONLY`.

The requested Fable/Sol review used the frozen evidence packet with SHA-256
`903ee5d60f3809f016901bb867ef6f067242f71cb6d7141877a22834b57ac81d`.
Fable returned HTTP 429 before consuming or producing any tokens, so that
branch is recorded as an `operational-only` provider stop. Root then ran the
established Claude Opus fallback on the exact same packet. Opus and
`gpt-5.6-sol` completed initial reviews and one reciprocal-critique round; the
report has SHA-256
`0736e8370a5a612f308e3ad225518deef3163580f2f6bbbe6d6de4b3a81721f7`.
Both final reviews recommend `LEDGER_ONLY` for all five proposals. Root checked
and narrowed every material objection in
`runs/agents/20260821-batch009-portfolio-admission/root-adjudication.md`.

The final dispositions are:

1. **B009-I01: ledger-only.** The primary radial-system source validates the
   important-hull anchor classification, but the panel contains no
   authenticated admissible sector on a decomposition-prime target-free
   realization and cannot distinguish radial structure from decomposable
   success. Reconsider after fixing the cyclic convention, producing one such
   positive control, and adding the affine-bad family. No `FP-01.D` is added.
2. **B009-I02: ledger-only.** The clone operation, potential, and deep-block
   terminal are not literal, and neither cited source transfers target safety
   or recursive normalization. Reconsider from a complete frozen operation
   plus one independently checked safe strict clone on a nonengineered prime
   target-free realization. No `FP-04` child is added.
3. **B009-I03: ledger-only.** The signed grading and atom tables are schematic,
   permit post-terminal feature selection, and lack a typed hole-to-terminal
   adapter. Reconsider only from a prospectively frozen source-only grading,
   controls, and adapter. No `FP-02.G` is added.
4. **B009-I04: ledger-only.** Fixed Boolean ideals are exact, but no covariant
   coloring-indexed OI object or constructive recoloring normal form exists in
   the packet. Reconsider from one nontrivial covariance trace and one semantic
   orbit reduction.
5. **B009-I05: ledger-only.** Ordered local recovery, heredity, star semantics,
   and a numeric grid-rank/width prediction are absent, while the consumer
   ladder introduces both target and support/split mechanisms. Reconsider from
   those definitions and one source-valid decoder identity. No `FP-13.F` is
   added.

No proposal is expired mathematically, because no fully specified mechanism
was decisively refuted. No `FP-NN` entry or child is admitted, no portfolio
state changes, no parent is revived, and no execution slate is selected.
