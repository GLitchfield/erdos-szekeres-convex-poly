# Ticket 0036: build an open-world research ecology with a grounded canary

## Question

Can a bounded research system preserve unconstrained framework invention,
remote mathematical transfers, and higher-dimensional generalizations while
also converting a meaningful fraction of those ideas into honest, replayable
first investigations?

This is not `FP-14.C` revival at admission and is not ELL v0.2.  Research
Factory v1, v2a, and ELL v0.1 remain terminal negative knowledge.  Their common
lesson is two-sided: generated semantic fields cannot certify question quality,
but a closed executable vocabulary can silently restrict the ideas the system
is capable of expressing.  This experiment tests a different mechanism:
open-world divergence followed by explicit, lineage-preserving decomposition
into grounded, bridge, and wild research objects.

Unlike ELL, this ticket does not ask whether evolutionary allocation beats a
control arm and does not require every generated atom to be executable.  Its
object is the transition between honestly distinct epistemic tiers: preserving
an unconstrained parent while measuring whether independently sourced ideas
can yield defined descendants and discriminating first investigations.  Reuse
of operator names or islands is implementation memory, not evidence for ELL's
abandoned arm-comparison question.  No OWRE outcome bears on that comparison.

## Non-negotiable design commitments

- The primitive registry is an extensible semantic cache, not an allowed
  vocabulary.  A lead may introduce new objects, predicates, representations,
  or proof mechanisms.
- `grounded`, `bridge`, and `wild` are epistemic statuses, not pre-answer
  quality labels.  No tier is a promotion state.
- Grounding creates a descendant and never overwrites, repairs, or deletes the
  original wild idea.
- Missing bridges are legitimate research targets.  Their missing status must
  be explicit and cannot be counted as if the bridge existed.
- Different tiers receive different first investigations and do not share a
  scalar fitness score.
- Primary-source provenance authenticates remote machinery; it does not prove
  that the machinery transfers to the Erdős--Szekeres setting.
- Lead events remain search information.  Only a later, separately approved
  compiling Lean theorem can move a destination dependency.
- A positive canary permits only a separately reviewed larger process
  experiment.  It does not revive `FP-14.C`, promote a mathematical route, or
  change `D-UPPER`, `D-HALF`, `D-DECOMP`, or `D-NORMALIZE`.

## Open representation graph

The representation graph begins with, but is not limited to:

1. planar point configurations and exact rational realizations;
2. rank-three and higher-rank chirotopes and oriented matroids;
3. allowable sequences and permutation encodings;
4. convex polytopes, circuits, cocircuits, and Gale diagrams;
5. convex geometries, closure systems, and antimatroid-style encodings;
6. ordered hypergraphs and Ramsey structures;
7. posets, recurrences, and proof-dependency graphs; and
8. newly introduced models carrying explicit definitions and provenance.

Every edge records direction, source and target types, transported structure,
and one of `project_proved`, `source_cited`, `computable`, `conjectural`, or
`known_false`.  A conjectural or absent edge is not a rejection.  It becomes a
bridge obligation with a smallest proposed test.  New nodes and edges are
allowed throughout divergence, but their epistemic status cannot be upgraded
without a frozen certificate.

## Three lead populations

### Grounded

Every object, predicate, generator, and observable used by the first probe has
defined semantics.  The lead includes an executable bounded probe and replay
recipe.  Grounded status says nothing about likely mathematical value.

### Bridge or transfer

The source machinery and destination question are defined, but their proposed
correspondence is not established.  The lead separates the authenticated
source theorem, proposed mapping, exact mismatch, smallest bridge obligation,
and a bounded translation, finite-shadow, or obstruction test.

### Wild framework

The lead may introduce a new invariant, dimensional lift, category change,
proof architecture, or remote analogy.  It is retained even when no current
interpreter covers it.  Its first investigation must attempt one of: precise
definition engineering, construction of a finite shadow, authentication of a
remote mechanism, or isolation of the first falsifiable bridge.  Mere prose
elaboration is not a lead event.

Tier transitions are descendants in the append-only lineage graph.  A wild
lead may yield several incompatible bridge descendants; a bridge lead may
become grounded or acquire a checked obstruction; and a grounded lead may
spawn new wild abstractions.

## Machinery atlas

Version 0.1 authenticates at least 24 substantive dossiers across roughly
eight neighboring areas.  Each dossier records:

- an original paper, publisher page, arXiv record, or official source;
- retrieval date and stable identifier;
- actual mathematical objects and dimensional parameters;
- exact theorem schema, hypotheses, and conclusion;
- proof mechanism or invariant at a useful level of detail;
- what transfers literally and what currently does not;
- proposed project representations and bridge obligations; and
- the cheapest meaningful falsifier or finite shadow.

Each authenticated dossier includes pinpoint primary-source passages for the
typed theorem schema and at least one proof-critical mechanism dependency.  An
independent reader reconstructs the objects, dimensions, hypotheses,
conclusion, mechanism, literal-transfer boundary, mismatch, and falsifier from
those passages.  Frozen hypothesis-deletion and dimension/type corruptions
must be rejected.  This reconstruction is required for every sampled bridge
lead and every dossier used by a counted source-distant event; citation
existence or internal metadata consistency alone is insufficient.

Candidate areas include oriented matroids, higher-dimensional convexity,
topological combinatorics, extremal hypergraphs and containers, convex closure
systems, probabilistic and entropy methods, algebraic or spectral methods,
and algorithmic or optimization formulations.  Quotas enforce breadth but do
not claim these areas are exhaustive.  Shallow keyword analogies fail dossier
authentication and remain in the raw idea archive rather than disappearing.

## Divergence and genetic operators

The frozen generation contains exactly 1,200 lineages: 100 in each of the
twelve tier-by-island strata.  The four islands are:

1. proof-graph composition and existing exact-route obstructions;
2. geometric, representation, and dimensional changes;
3. authenticated transferred machinery; and
4. high-variance or deliberately strange frameworks.

Micro operators change premises, conclusions, parameters, witnesses, and
observables.  Meso operators mutate representation edges, bridge directions,
finite shadows, and source--consumer compositions.  Macro operators perform
dimensional lift, dualization, model change, proof-engine transfer, obstruction
inversion, framework crossover, and extraction of failed bridges as standalone
questions.

Typed compatibility is mandatory only for claims labeled executable.  Wild
crossovers may introduce a new symbol, but the symbol ledger must record its
definition status and provenance.  Binary crossover must consume both parents.
Surface rewording, tier labels, operator names, and arm markers do not count as
structural novelty.  The immutable genome retains the original statement,
introduced objects, assumptions, source ancestry, unresolved atoms,
tier-specific first investigation, and complete operator trace.

Before any 1,200-lineage generation, the primitive/interpreter version and an
independently authored checker are frozen.  The checker must pass positive,
negative, missing-consumer, trivial-shadow, wrong-tier, corrupted-provenance,
outcome-preserving mutation, and malformed-certificate fixtures.  At least one
replay-valid grounded probe with positive and negative controls must then run
in each of the four islands.  Failure stops as `OWRE-REVIEW-REJECT`; scale is
not used to debug the semantic substrate.

Whole-population structural fingerprints ignore prose, tier, operator, seed,
and other administrative fields while retaining mathematical objects,
representations, unresolved atoms, source ancestry, and consumers.  Duplicate
fingerprints may comprise at most ten percent of a stratum.  No immediate
ancestor, machinery dossier, or mechanism family may supply more than 25
percent of a stratum.  Effective cluster counts are reported for every
stratum.  Any violation is `OWRE-SUBSTRATE-DEFECT` with no backfill.

## Independent audit

Before the canary, independent auditors inspect a frozen stratified sample of
144 lineages, twelve from every tier-by-island stratum, plus all later canary
selections.  All leads labeled grounded receive exhaustive executable-semantic
validation.  The audit checks provenance, tier honesty, bridge status,
introduced-symbol accounting, genealogy, structural novelty, and the absence
of hidden target conclusions or stored witnesses.

A material error is one of: fabricated or incorrect source attribution;
incorrect theorem schema or proof-mechanism reconstruction; a false executable
claim; wrong epistemic tier; an unstated or falsely upgraded bridge; missing
introduced-symbol provenance; false genealogy or structural fingerprint; or a
target/stored-witness flow hidden from the record.  A lineage with one or more
material errors counts once.  Two auditors classify every sampled lineage
without reading the other's decision.  Root adjudicates disagreements using
only pinpoint evidence frozen by the auditors; an unresolved disagreement is
counted as defective.  Seeded-defect sensitivity fixtures must be detected
before the real sample is opened.

Any fabricated citation or false executable claim triggers
`OWRE-INTEGRITY-STOP`.  At least eight defective lineages among the 144 sampled
lineages, or at least three defective lineages within any twelve-lineage
stratum, triggers `OWRE-AUDIT-MISMATCH`.  Canary defects are scored separately
under a zero-defect rule.  There is no repair, replacement, or backfill after
the sample is opened.

The audit does not judge whether a question is promising, elegant, or true.

## Twenty-four-lead canary

The canary has exactly 24 leads: two selected uniformly by a frozen
outcome-blind seed from the audit-eligible population in every tier-by-island
stratum.  Selection uses only tier, island, eligibility, diversity caps, and
provenance availability; it cannot inspect perceived promise, a proposed
consumer's importance, or any probe outcome.  There are no evolutionary or
control arms and no arm-effect claim.  The canary tests whether the open-world
substrate can reach honest investigation across all three tiers.

- Grounded leads execute their exact finite or interface probe.
- Bridge leads test their smallest translation, finite shadow, or obstruction.
- Wild leads attempt definition engineering, a finite shadow, source
  authentication, or isolation of a falsifiable bridge.

Every lead receives one terminal record under its frozen cap.  A replay-valid
lead event must start from a prospectively frozen uncertainty and a test with
at least two possible outcomes, positive and negative controls, and an
outcome-specific certificate accepted by the already qualified independent
checker.  The observed result must toggle a named premise, consumer, bridge
obligation, or previously undecided portfolio disposition, or strictly reduce
a recorded bridge obligation under a frozen measure.  This requirement applies
to every tier.  Vacuous scales, constant or trivial shadows, definition-only
artifacts, already-known outcomes, restated analogies, and retirement for mere
undefinedness, timeout, or a mismatch already stated in the dossier do not
count.

For wild leads, a definition can count only when it additionally yields an
executable discriminating shadow, a checked nontrivial inconsistency, or a
precise bridge consumed downstream.  Source authentication by itself is not an
event.

`OWRE-CANARY-SIGNAL` requires all of:

- 24 of 24 honest terminal records;
- no provenance, tier, selection, ancestry, or post-outcome replacement defect;
- at least 18 completed tier-appropriate first investigations;
- at least six independently replayable lead events;
- at least one counted event from each epistemic tier;
- at least three mechanism families among counted events; and
- at least two counted events derived from source-distant machinery dossiers.

At most two counted events may come from one mechanism family.  Evidence
vectors remain descriptive and tier-specific; no scalar aggregation selects
the terminal.

## Outcome contract

- `OWRE-REVIEW-REJECT`: adversarial review finds a closed ontology, subjective
  goodness filter, gameable event definition, or correlated comparison that
  cannot be repaired before generation, or the frozen checker and four-island
  grounded smoke gate do not pass before scale.
- `OWRE-SUBSTRATE-DEFECT`: representation, lineage, tier, operator, replay, or
  schema contracts cannot be satisfied without weakening them.
- `OWRE-AUDIT-MISMATCH`: the frozen semantic/provenance sample exceeds its
  material-error threshold without backfill.
- `OWRE-INTEGRITY-STOP`: any fabricated citation, false executable claim,
  ancestry leakage, unblinded review, or post-outcome repair appears.
- `OWRE-RESOURCE-STOP`: a frozen wall, CPU, memory, or output cap prevents the
  terminal canary.
- `OWRE-CANARY-NO-SIGNAL`: integrity holds but the exact signal conditions do
  not all hold.
- `OWRE-CANARY-SIGNAL`: all exact signal conditions hold and a separate larger
  process experiment may be proposed.

Terminal precedence is review rejection; resource stop; integrity stop;
substrate or audit defect; no signal; then signal.  Every outcome has neutral
proof movement.  Negative evidence does not promote a refined descendant, and
a signal does not itself revive `FP-14.C`.

## Execution slate

This is exploration with zero proof-producing and three information-gathering
assignments:

1. representation graph and primary-source machinery atlas;
2. three-tier divergence, genetic operators, decomposition, and lineage
   memory; and
3. independent semantic audit and arm-blind canary investigation.

All slots are intentionally concentrated in one research-process mechanism
family.  Root owns randomization, shared contracts, execution slates, terminal
scoring, timing, bibliography, and Wayfinder reconciliation.

## Resources and approvals

Forecast is 180--360 wall minutes with a hard seven-hour stop.  Each local
process is capped at 30 minutes; aggregate resources are eight CPU cores,
16 GiB RAM, and 5 GiB under ignored run directories.  The approved block
authorizes one exact Sol/Fable provider review, primary-source web search and
citation chaining, three specialist assignments, bounded local Python,
ignored run artifacts, ticket 0036, timing and bibliography updates, and final
Wayfinder reconciliation.

It does not authorize Lean/Lake, solver scale-up, software installation,
destructive action, commit, push, third-party code import, or external
repository mutation.  Papers remain outside the repository unless provenance
and license justify a separately approved snapshot.  Any mathematical lead
warranting formalization receives a later proof packet.

## Decision criteria

Run no population until the prospectively independent checker suite and one
real replay-valid grounded smoke with positive and negative controls pass in
each island.  Admit no atlas or lineage merely because its producer's internal
tests pass.  After population freeze, continue only if both blind auditors stay
below the exact global and per-stratum defect stops; then select exactly two
canary leads per tier-by-island stratum without promise or outcome access.
Apply the terminal precedence and no-repair/no-backfill rules above exactly.

## Resolution

`OWRE-REVIEW-REJECT`.

The fresh adversarial review returned `MODIFY`, and its required open-world,
anti-triviality, source-reconstruction, concentration, and blind-audit
corrections were frozen before any worker output.  The three information lanes
then completed independently.  The atlas produced 24 proposal-level machinery
dossiers across eight areas.  The substrate and audit lanes each passed their
own prospective synthetic tests, with the checker suite passing all 35 frozen
corruptions and threshold controls.  None of those internal outcomes is a
cross-admission result.

The first frozen cross-compatibility command rejected the substrate
representation graph immediately:

```text
ContractError: nodes[0].definition_status is not recognized
```

The producer used `project_defined`, `substrate_defined`, and
`open_extension`; the independently frozen checker used `defined`,
`source_cited`, `computable`, `open_obligation`, `conjectural`, and
`known_false`.  The root representation contract required the field but had
not prospectively frozen its vocabulary.  The no-adapter/no-repair manifest
therefore stopped before the four real grounded smokes and cancelled the
1,200-lineage population, 144-lineage audit, and 24-lead canary.  Separate
post-terminal structural checks also rejected the atlas dossier envelope and
representation graph against the same independent checker.  No output was
translated, patched, replaced, or backfilled after these observations.

This exact execution is expired.  The atlas remains reusable proposal/search
information, not admitted machinery and not a route promotion.  The smallest
necessary successor prerequisite is a single prospectively frozen canonical
interchange fixture covering every enum, typed unresolved atom, all three
tier records, unary and binary genealogy, fingerprint canonicalization,
population/smoke envelopes, and dossier reconstruction.  Producer and checker
must independently pass that fixture before domain authoring.  That repair is
necessary but not sufficient to schedule a descendant and does not reset the
research-question mechanism family's negative-evidence streak.

**Net proof movement:** neutral.  **Evidence:** proposal-level atlas and source
packages, 35 checked synthetic validator cases, and a checked frozen
cross-compatibility failure.  **Difficulty displaced:** no mathematical
obligation changed; the process obstruction is narrowed to a prospective
interchange contract, but no executable lead or event was produced.
**Counterfactual:** knowing the mismatch earlier would have saved authoring
time but would not shorten the Erdős--Szekeres proof or improve its success
probability.  **Planning consequence:** expire OWRE v0.1, promote no dossier,
edge, lead, or descendant, keep `FP-14.C` suspended, and require a separately
reviewed mechanism before any new process experiment.  `D-UPPER`, `D-HALF`,
`D-DECOMP`, and `D-NORMALIZE` remain unchanged; no load-bearing edge changed.
