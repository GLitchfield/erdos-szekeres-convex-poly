## Question

Can the exact 32-vertex state turn one of the four compiled residual
obstructions into a target or a strict parameter predecessor: lower-localized
escape, upper-localized escape, a residual cup disjoint from a universal
minimum-column cap, or a four-owner private residual star at that column?

## Frozen inputs

- the full residual no-opposite-extensions theorem;
- `LowerLocalizedResidualEscape578` and
  `UpperLocalizedResidualEscape578`;
- `MinimumColumnPrivateResidualStar578` and its exact singleton intersections;
- the 24-entry deletion ledger, one-spine density, and six disjoint cap
  blocks; and
- the exact-small projected countermodels from Experiments 0079 and 0082.

## Decision criteria

- A localized consumer must use the escaping residual cup and all six cap
  columns, not merely the `24+8` cardinal split.
- A minimum-column consumer must use either the disjoint residual cup or all
  four singleton-owned deletion witnesses.  All-base addresses alone are
  already refuted.
- A recursive result must name the strictly smaller `(a,u,k)` predecessor and
  prove every inherited avoidance.  Proper support containment alone is not
  enough.
- Falsify each proposed consumer against the existing exact-small models and
  a bounded full-state quotient before a large proof.

## Candidate outcomes

1. A compiling consumer closes the exact color-dual singleton gate.
2. A compiling theorem eliminates at least one of the four branches and
   leaves one named obstruction.
3. An exact target-free coloring retains the full proposed consumer; expire
   this matrix route without claiming state `(5,7,8)` false.

## Resolution

Outcome 2. The universal-column private-star branch now contains a full
six-column plateau deletion row and two distinct foreign cap blocks disjoint
from that row. Lean proves this in
`StructuralChargingNextState578PlateauForeignCaps.lean` by combining all four
singleton owners, the no-opposite-extensions law, exact lowest-split
nonlocality, and all six disjoint cap columns.

This does not close state `(5,7,8)`. The localized escape branches and the
disjoint universal-column cup remain, while the raw private-star alternative
is replaced by one named plateau/two-foreign-cap obstruction. Address-only
plateau consumers remain refuted by the existing relaxed full-matrix model.
Ticket 0010 owns the next color-sensitive consumer.

The result remains conditional on the existing `hone` hypothesis used to
construct the one-spine 32-point package. It does not discharge `hone` or
cover a multi-spine branch. The bounded pre-implementation review identified
this qualification and rejected any inference from ledger injectivity to
pairwise-disjoint witness cups; the compiling plateau theorem uses neither
inference.
