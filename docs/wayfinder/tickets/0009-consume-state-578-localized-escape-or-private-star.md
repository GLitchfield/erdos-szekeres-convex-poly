Can the exact 32-vertex state turn one of the four compiled residual
obstructions into a target or a strict parameter predecessor: lower-localized
escape, upper-localized escape, a residual cup disjoint from a universal
minimum-column cap, or a four-owner private residual star at that column?

  `UpperLocalizedResidualEscape578`;

## Decision criteria

- A localized consumer must use the escaping residual cup and all six cap
  columns, not merely the `24+8` cardinal split.
- A minimum-column consumer must use either the disjoint residual cup or all
  four singleton-owned deletion witnesses.  All-base addresses alone are
  already refuted.
- A recursive result must name the strictly smaller `(a,u,k)` predecessor and
  prove every inherited avoidance.  Proper support containment alone is not
  enough.
- Falsify each proposed consumer against the existing exact-small models and
  a bounded full-state quotient before a large proof.

## Candidate outcomes

1. A compiling consumer closes the exact color-dual singleton gate.
2. A compiling theorem eliminates at least one of the four branches and
   leaves one named obstruction.
3. An exact target-free coloring retains the full proposed consumer; expire
