## Decision criteria

1. Authenticate every Cycle 003 terminal against the immutable manifest before
   admitting any mathematical claim.
2. Charge failures to the exact representation tested.  In particular, do not
   transfer an individual-extension counterexample to the source's stronger
   common-outer-polygon lemma, and do not treat lossless full-tree replay as a
   mechanism-wide refutation of contextual decomposable invariants.

   source's cyclic family of several cores qualifies; a larger bucket count,

   strict measure.  A clean conditional shell theorem without a live consumer
   remains useful source information but is not theorem entry.
5. Use the smaller honest slate.  Four is an occupancy target, not permission
   to pad two correlated audits with duplicate work.

## Cycle 003 reconciliation

The two decomposable lanes reach the same honest boundary by different exact
representations.  Full divergence words and full cut traces are useful semantic
normal forms, but their only automatic recursion decreases evaluation size,
not an extremal measure.  A successor would need a separately motivated
forbidden subtrace or quotient law with a collision payoff and a Pascal
capacity theorem.  No such fact is present, so another immediate
`D-DECOMP` descendant is not selected.

The single-core nested-hull reading also stops.  It cannot cover a general
target-free carrier, and the Fin15 anchors neither share one core nor satisfy
the false individual-to-joint extension implication.  The source nevertheless
contains one mathematically different observation not tested in Cycle 003:
Lemma 2 uses a cyclic family `P_i` of several cores whose exposed edges cover
the vertices of an outer polygon.  If a no-convex-`r` version is valid, its
natural candidate inequality is

```text
card V(P_0) <= sum_i (r - card V(P_i) - 1).
```

Unlike the failed total single-core carrier, this representation changes the
core with the outer sector while preserving a global cyclic cover.  It is an
exact source-backed decomposition and therefore justifies one bounded probe
under the project's exploratory-patience rule.  It is not yet a theorem-entry
memo: a canonical producer of the `P_i`, a nonvacuous strict measure, and the
return to the alternating-layer half support are unproved.

### Probing account

- **Concrete observation:** the source covers every outer vertex by an
  exactly-one-edge region of at least one member of a cyclic *multi-core*
  family, then sums local bucket capacities.
- **Live explanation A:** the proof is parameter-uniform and yields a genuine
  outer-shell budget once a cyclic family is produced.
- **Live explanation B:** the general inequality is formally true but its
  easiest fan-triangle specialization is numerically vacuous, so the useful
  fixed-six force comes only from specially produced quadrilateral and
  pentagonal cores.
- **Live explanation C:** an ordinary convex-layer or alternating-layer
  construction canonically supplies enough large cores to constrain a
  predecessor crossing layers.
- **Live explanation D:** the source family is a case-analysis artifact with
  no canonical producer from arbitrary layers, leaving both the bridge and
  the half-support return speculative.
- **Cheapest discriminator:** derive the exact parameterized shell statement,
  then instantiate the most canonical hull-layer fans and list the
  producer/consumer obligations before and after.  No computation or Lean is
  needed.
- **Incubation boundary:** two static lanes, 60 minutes each, one immutable
  result apiece.  Stop the entire observation after this cycle unless a full
  five-field memo or a source-authenticated nonvacuous intermediate consumer
  survives.
