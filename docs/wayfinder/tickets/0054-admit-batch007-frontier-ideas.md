# Ticket 0054: admit or reject Batch 007 frontier ideas

## Question

Which of the five Batch 007 proposals should be merged into an existing stable
mechanism family, admitted as a new frontier entry, retained only in the idea
ledger, or expired before any next execution slate is selected?

## Decision criteria

Each admitted proposal must have a precise model tier, named destination
consumer, scope ceiling, missing generalization bridge, concrete supporting
observation, bounded discriminating gate, outcome branches, and an expiry or
revival rule. Shared mathematical objects, unresolved producers, and recorded
counterexamples determine correlation even when a proposal uses new
vocabulary.

An exact representation plus a cheap falsifier may support bounded `probing`
only when the gate varies mathematical information and distinguishes live
explanations. A finite survivor, local structural lemma, literature analogy,
or conditional interface is neutral search information. It cannot revive a
suspended uniform parent or sustain `advancing` without a recent compiling
theorem that closes a named dependency.

New main entries are admitted conservatively. A proposal is merged when its
consumer and failure mode are already owned by an existing stable family. It
remains ledger-only when its key premise is strategically equivalent to the
target, its transfer is undefined, or its first gate cannot exercise the
proposed mechanism. Expiry requires a decisive contradiction or duplication,
not merely a low prior probability.

This ticket resolves portfolio disposition only. It does not select or approve
an execution slate.

## Resolution

Outcome: `BATCH007-LEDGER-ONLY`.

The exact Fable/Sol command was launched once on the frozen packet. Codex Sol
completed a high-confidence structured review. Claude Fable 5 authenticated
but returned a zero-token HTTP 429 because its usage limit was exhausted, so
the harness stopped before reciprocal critique. This is an
`operational-only` provider event, not a mathematical vote. The identical
packet then completed the repository's established Claude Opus fallback plus
Codex Sol with one reciprocal-critique round. Both final reviewers recommend
no portfolio admission or merge.

Root independently checked the cited repository passages and the actual Lean
definitions of `TurnSequenceAllowed` and `OneChangeOn`. The complete
middle-link family is lossless, and the direct checkerboard/lonesum argument
is supported by the eight allowed words. That exact local observation does not
repair the undefined cross-middle hierarchy or make it a compiling theorem.
The final dispositions are:

1. **B007-I01: ledger-only.** Exact repair restores the predecessor-free
   common support that suspended `FP-01` already lacks. Reconsider only after
   an independently produced selection or repair lemma strictly reduces that
   obligation; finite list optimization is insufficient.
2. **B007-I02: ledger-only.** The lossless representation and local lonesum
   law remain live, and `FP-13.E`'s omitted-bit counterexample does not transfer.
   Before admission, freeze a choice-free cross-middle construction, parent
   maps, root count, and decoder, then exhibit one nontrivial positive control.
   No `FP-13.F` or new top-level entry is added.
3. **B007-I03: ledger-only.** Without an authenticated equal-support collision,
   the lacing mechanism cannot run; a collision-free census is
   nondiscriminating. A future success would be a correlated producer for
   Batch 006 I02, not an independent route.
4. **B007-I04: ledger-only.** There is no premise-complete balanced input,
   provenance-forced nonidentity loop, or natural length bound. Opus proposed
   candidate-local expiry because the frozen edges may force identity
   monodromy; Sol noted that this vacuity is unproved. Claim discipline keeps
   the card ledger-only until a formal symbolic audit decides that point. No
   `FP-02.G` is added, and this card review does not discharge `FP-02`'s broader
   mandatory route comparison.
5. **B007-I05: ledger-only.** Distinctness plus rank at most `k-2` currently
   restates `D-UPPER` in affine-code language. Reconsider only after a
   `OneChangeOn` identity produces a nontrivial affine dependency on a
   nonrecursive realizable control family.

No new `FP-NN` entry or child is admitted, no parent state changes, no parent is
revived, and no execution slate is selected.
