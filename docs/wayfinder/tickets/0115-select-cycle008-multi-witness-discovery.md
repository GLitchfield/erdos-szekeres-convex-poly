# Ticket 0115: select Cycle 008 multi-witness discovery

## Question

no qualified layer, cut, or source memo, is there a materially different
intermediary theorem whose producer and consumer can both be named before a
Lean builder starts?

## Decision criteria

1. Vary the mathematical information rather than enlarge an instance, patch a
   premise, rename an expired support predicate, or restore a lossless state.
2. Require every lane to return the five theorem-entry fields below or an
   explicit bounded stop; a true but inert adjacent lemma does not qualify.
3. Keep at most two slots in one mechanism family and classify all four slots
   as information-gathering until a theorem contract survives reconciliation.
4. Use current compiling producers and consumers.  A new bridge and a new
   connection from that bridge to a load-bearing consumer cannot both remain
   speculative.
5. Admit negative evidence only at the narrowest tested scope, never promote a
   correlated descendant because a sibling failed, and create a failure genome
   only for an independently admitted mathematical negative.
6. Preserve the paused scheduler and failed/stopped idea-generator state, and
   prohibit Lean, computation, providers, network access, and canonical edits
   inside the four static lanes.

## Selection rationale

The repository contains an underused exact seam in
`StructuralChargingResidueTransitions.lean`.  For two strong one-vertex
extensions of a common five-point residue, the no-strong-seven and one-change
hypotheses give an exact two-state classification: a first-middle extension
disagreement occurs exactly in the constant external-pair-profile branch.
This retains a relation between multiple witnesses, unlike the expired
single-support consumer.  No repository theorem currently consumes that
classification, so it is a discovery candidate rather than an advancing
route.

A second independent observation comes from
`StructuralChargingResidualProbeGeneric.lean`.  Its aggregate seed-cover
count uses only that every `(B-1)`-seed lies in a bad `B`-block.  Holding a
fixed `(B-2)`-seed instead shows that its `R-B+2` one-point extensions must be
covered, while one containing `B`-block accounts for at most two extensions.
Thus a pointwise lower-codegree statement, rather than another aggregate
cardinality bound, is available for hostile consumer audit.  Whether the
existing residual geometry consumes that extra overlap is not yet known.

The fourth lane is deliberately outside both families.  It asks whether the
explicit recursive lower construction has a small, non-target-equivalent
one-point stability lemma.  This is foundational probing: unless it has a
single named bridge to an existing load-bearing consumer, it receives only a
small incubation and cannot be called proof movement.

No advancing high-leverage entry is deliberately deferred: the fresh
portfolio contains no `advancing` entry and no currently executable theorem
gate.  `FP-01`, `FP-02`, `FP-03`, `FP-05`, and `FP-13` remain gated;
`FP-04` and the other suspended families lack their stated revival evidence.

## Required five-field memo

A qualified terminal must provide all of the following:

1. a literal Lean-shaped theorem or exact falsifier at an explicit model tier;
2. a current producer using named repository hypotheses rather than the desired
   conclusion under a synonym;
3. a current downstream consumer and a ladder with at most one speculative
   arrow after the proposed structural theorem;
4. unresolved obligations before and after, together with a strict decrease in
   hypothesis strength, quantifier complexity, open cases, certificate size,
   or dependency depth; and
5. the cheapest hostile control, stop branches, budget, and evidence gate.

An attractive identity, proof sketch, true normalization lemma, pigeonhole
fact, or finite analogy without these fields is search information only.  A
negative in one common-residue lane cannot promote the other lane or a refined
descendant.
