## Question

Can the already formalized one-region Suk geometry be extended through an
authenticated Pór--Valtr input to an actual finite global alternative, without
assuming the chain-rich or antichain-rich branch that the argument must prove?

The target was deliberately intermediate.  A successful block had to compile
the recurrence and the source-to-raw-pool transport, then derive either actual
nonadjacent antichain witnesses or an actual consecutive run of chain witnesses.
It did not claim Suk's positive-fraction theorem, asymptotic upper bound, or any
exact Erdős--Szekeres implication.

## Decision criteria

A theorem counted only if it compiled from a frozen source-shaped contract and
closed a named dependency consumed later in the same ladder.  Source audits,
finite controls, and rejected adapters could change allocation but were not
proof movement.  The global endpoint could not assume either branch, and no
result could be credited to the exact conjecture without a proved implication
into the load-bearing ledger.

## Frozen discipline

Every Lean task froze its theorem before controls and proof search, used an
independent finite checker, serialized the builder lane, ran a targeted module
build, and audited public declaration axioms.  No task could add a selected
chain, antichain, convex subset, target support membership, recurrence outcome,
or asymptotic conclusion as a premise after seeing a control.

The Pór--Valtr audit kept source correction and theorem repair separate.  The
paper's open region supplies `ceil (N / 2^(32*r)) - 1` interior points; the full
ceiling appears only after adjoining a boundary anchor.  The literal x-sorted
adapter was therefore tested and rejected without treating the cyclic source
mechanism as refuted.

audit, exact rational controls, and independent finite checkers.

strictly downstream obligations rather than renamed premises of the compiled
theorems.

**Counterfactual:** knowing the compiling results before the block would reduce
the remaining quantitative proof plan to the source producer and two explicit
branch consumers.  Knowing only the source mismatch or the failed x-sorted
adapter would change allocation but not reduce proof work.

**Planning consequence:** retain `FP-15.B` as advancing at the chain-run
consumer, plan the antichain-union consumer in parallel, and keep `FP-15.C`
locked.  Do not begin asymptotic optimization before both branch consumers and
the source-data producer compile.

No load-bearing destination dependency changed.  `D-UPPER`, `D-HALF`,
`D-DECOMP`, and `D-NORMALIZE` remain open; `D-ASSEMBLE` remains gated;
