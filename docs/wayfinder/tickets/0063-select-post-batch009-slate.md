## Decision criteria

Apply the complete proof-distance, exploratory-patience, mechanism-correlation,
portfolio-admission, and slate-selection rules in `AGENTS.md`. Four is a
capacity target, not a quota. A new probing entry needs an exact representation
or concrete unexplained structure, a bounded discriminating gate, exact stop
conditions, and a short consumer ladder. A suspended parent cannot be revived
by a cheap falsifier or an unobserved analogy. A proof-producing slot must
close or strictly reduce a named dependency and state its scope ceiling.

Admission, reconciliation, validation, and selection occur serially:

1. adjudicate all four cards against the bounded review;
2. update every admitted/affected portfolio record and the idea ledger;
3. run `python3 scripts/check_frontier_portfolio.py`; and
4. select only from the resulting valid registry.

## Resolution

`POST-B009-SLATE-SELECTED`.

The Fable/Sol review completed on the frozen evidence packet, and an additional
same-packet Opus/Sol review—launched while the still-live Fable process was
hidden by a sandbox-level process view—was allowed to finish. Both reciprocal
reviews converge after critique. Provider output remains advisory; root
independently checked the Lean interfaces and the primary TeX.

The preliminary two-lane hypothesis is revised to one lane:

1. admit the exact arbitrary-eleven-window transport as correlated main bridge
   `FP-17`, rather than silently extending completed `FP-01.A`, and select it
   as one proof-producing `WIN11` lane;
2. keep `SUPPORT-ATLAS` ledger-only under suspended `FP-01`; its adjacent
   predicate is automatic outside the unique exchanged-point cases, its panel
   reuses prior controls, and it has no positive global-support mechanism;
3. keep B001-I03 `HANKEL` ledger-only until an explicit `k`-bounded shuffle
   feature algebra or genuinely new context discriminator exists; and
4. keep B003-I05 `BETA` ledger-only because the frozen source sign and cone
   fail the exact four-point one-interior check. The no-repair contract forbids
   correcting it in place; a consistent successor needs a fresh route review.

`WIN11` must prove an arbitrary-support ambient-to-restricted strong-set
reflection, including injective preimage/cardinality bookkeeping, or an
equivalent mapped-fan argument. Forward transport alone cannot establish its
ambient no-strong-five conclusion. If the theorem compiles, the subordinate
obligation set decreases from

```text
{arbitrary-support transport, window relabeling, compatible/global support}
```

to

```text
{compatible/global support}.
```

That future result would be forward only at the fixed-`k=6` bridge scope. The
current admission and selection are neutral. The final slate has one
proof-producing and zero information-gathering slots in one mechanism family;
the concentration is justified because no other admitted registry entry has a
dispatch-ready gate. No `advancing` entry is deferred.
