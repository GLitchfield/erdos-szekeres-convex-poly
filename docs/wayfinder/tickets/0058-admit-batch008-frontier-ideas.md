The frozen evidence packet, SHA-256
`5bb94fdfd13e2a5c25ee4a27a153b571342dc4edc31db0e90e5072098096b5b9`,

`runs/agents/20260821-batch008-portfolio-admission/root-adjudication.md`.

The final dispositions are:

1. **B008-I01: ledger-only.** The flip direction, start convention, and
   fan/triangulation coupling are not frozen, and there is no observed legal
   geometric escape.  Reconsider from a sourced directed flip object plus one
   authenticated blocked-fan escape.  The ceiling is fixed `k=6`.
2. **B008-I02: ledger-only.** The strategy-to-half consumer is exact, but the
   Breaker theorem strictly strengthens the desired support and has no
   observed win or orientation-sensitive response producer.  Reconsider from
   a nondecomposable realizable win and a genuinely dynamic invariant.
3. **B008-I03: ledger-only.** The structural theorem bundles ray existence,
   strict split, realizable filling, and zero-safety, while the exact fan
   enumeration is unimplemented and uncalibrated.  Reconsider from one fully
   checked signed split ray and a complete enumeration contract.
4. **B008-I04: ledger-only.** The 1,540-sign splice is valid and post-SAT
   compliant, but no one-sided summary updates or valid mixed-splice corpus
   with both outputs is frozen.  Reconsider only after those facts exist.  No
   `FP-02.G` is added.
5. **B008-I05: ledger-only.** Most clauses 1--2 are definitional or generic
   event-domain consequences; a finite pass would not test the wall-to-strong
   decoder, and the B006-I03 duplicate check is absent.  Reconsider from an
   exact common-control nonduplication comparison and a nonautomatic local
   identity reducing clause 3.  No `FP-13.F` is added.

No `FP-NN` entry or child is admitted, no portfolio state changes, no parent is
revived, and no execution slate is selected.
