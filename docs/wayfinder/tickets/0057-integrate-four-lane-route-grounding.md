### FAN13 quantifier and tier boundary

The frozen CNF is the exact negation of the universal theorem: it asserts one
premise-valid coloring and one pure fan for which all six exterior insertions
fail. All 24,024 fan variables and 144,144 insertion variables are
bi-implicationally derived from the 286 signs. The model's 532 compatible pairs
elsewhere are therefore consistent with, rather than contrary to, the
refutation. They also show that the weaker selective statement “some pure fan
extends” was not tested; that statement restores the suspended selector
obligation and is not promoted.

The one bounded coordinate search stopped with thirteen wrong signs and one
zero determinant. That miss says nothing about realizability. No
geometry-specific route is portfolio-ready. A future exact stretchability
decision for this frozen order type remains logically discriminating only if a
prospectively frozen certificate-producing method becomes independently
available; more coordinate seeds, grids, or retuning are not a new gate.

### EX10 vacuity boundary

The one authorized source-faithful recursive/deep-composition family is
consumed. Nine rotations have a four-cap and all ten have a strong eleven-set,
so there are zero complete-premise cases. This is a family-premise-vacuous miss,
not evidence for or against the literal exchange conclusion. The 980 old
Ticket 0050 cases used a weaker sampling premise; the 876 number is the count
of distinct orientation digests across all 4,112 old direct/reflected records,
