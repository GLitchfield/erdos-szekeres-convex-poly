## Cycle 004 reconciliation

The conditional shell theorem is mathematically clean but does not reduce the
destination obligation.  Before using it one must produce a cyclic family of
large, carrier-supported, interior-compatible cores.  After using it one must
still turn a shell-cardinality bound into an omitted-vertex extension for an
arbitrary strong predecessor crossing hull layers.  The number of speculative
bridges remains two and the known hard cross-layer orientation obligation is
not smaller.

right side is at least `3(r-4)>r-1`, while target-freeness already bounds every
convex hull by `r-1`.  Larger solver instances, more layers, or a premise that
postulates nearly maximal cores would vary resources or assume the producer,
not change the mathematical information.  The immediate multicore-shell
observation is therefore expired as a direct `D-HALF` candidate after its
predeclared final probe.
