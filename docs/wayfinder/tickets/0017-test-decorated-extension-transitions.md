## Question

Does the geometry of one- and two-point right extensions impose a useful
transition law on permutation-valued pair links once the color data needed by
the Suk and Holmsen--Mojarrad--Pach--Tardos spike arguments is retained?

The completed extension-fiber slate ruled out a bare fixed-arrangement wiring
as compression and ruled out abstract localization, connectivity, and a direct
majority consumer.  It did not test whether the exact two-step OneChange
coupling is a smaller decorated transition system with a source-backed gluing
consumer.

## Decision criteria

The bounded literature and Sol/Fable reviews support only a narrow probe.
Rank-two link orders, common connector blocks, spike conjugacy, and the exact
support-chain gluing mechanism are retained.  Bare Ferrers or RSK shape is not:
it loses the left/right and inner/outer labels needed by the published gluing
argument, and an arbitrary insertion need not give a contained one-box shape
update.  Full Greene--Kleitman formalization and blind seven-point enumeration
are therefore downstream-only.

The approved slate has four slots:

1. **Proof-producing, FP-13:** formalize the exact permutation/interval
   transition consequences of one and two right extensions.
2. **Information-gathering, FP-13:** freeze and test a nested decorated-summary
   ladder on the admitted through-six atlas.  A summary succeeds only if it
   omits atomic wiring data while determining both the relevant extension set
   and a named spike/gluing verdict.
3. **Proof-producing, FP-15.B:** formalize the transitive two-color cup--cap
   theorem on a `SukRegionLE` chain and freeze the global adjacent-chain
   selection statement feeding `SukAdjacentSupportData.toCertificate`.
4. **Proof-producing, FP-16:** audit and, only after exact semantic admission,
   begin the unrestricted `erdosSzekeresFormulaAt_six` proof architecture.

Two of four slots belong to the extension/spike mechanism family, satisfying
the default concentration limit.  The innovation check is **reuse current
result**: the two bounded reviews under
`runs/reviews/20260815-signotope-spike-route/` and
`runs/reviews/20260815-spike-ferrers-route/` already adversarially reviewed
this redirect.  Ordinary implementation does not need another provider call.

## Exact gates

### Transition theorem

Freeze conventions before proof search.  The target must state, using the
project's actual pair-link API, whether a compatible link is represented by a
permutation/inversion set and how the colors
`sigma i = chi i x y` constrain two consecutive links.  The expected interval
and blockwise inversion laws may not be weakened after a counterexample.  A
compiling theorem is reusable infrastructure; it changes a load-bearing edge
only when a named consumer uses it.

### Decorated-summary falsifier

Before outcomes, freeze a nested information ladder consisting of link order,
connector blocks, source-authenticated left/right and inner/outer labels, and
the smallest adjacent-spike gluing tag.  Test only the admitted realizations
and cells through child size six.  Identical summaries must imply identical
declared outputs.  The summary must omit at least one atomic-wiring datum on a
checked instance.  Otherwise the candidate is reconstruction rather than
compression and expires.  The seven-point outcome remains closed unless this
gate passes unchanged and exposes a short theorem-to-consumer ladder.

### Suk global selection

Prove the chain-level transitive cup--cap lemma first.  Freeze exact finite
cardinality inequalities and a global adjacent-chain selection proposition
before attempting the producer.  The existing support-data-to-certificate
theorem is the required consumer.  Repackaging the certificate, local sign
algebra, or asymptotic bookkeeping is not a successful outcome.

### Exact six

Map the primary and Isabelle/HOL semantics clause-by-clause to
`OneChangeOn`, `IsSweepRealizable`, strong convex position, the planar bridge,
and `ErdosSzekeresFormulaAt 6`.  Do not generate or replay a certificate until
that table supports a noncircular frozen Lean statement.  Eventual success
closes only subordinate edge `D-UPPER(6)`.

## Outcome and stop discipline

- A compiling transition theorem without a consumer is neutral proof movement
  but a successful formal bridge.
- A strictly smaller decorated state with a named consumer permits one blind
  seven-point gate and then a Lean adapter.  A state equivalent to full wiring
  expires the compression descendant without expiring every extension idea.
- A compiling global Suk producer moves the local quantitative route forward
  but does not change exact `D-UPPER` without a proved implication.
- A compiling unrestricted six theorem closes `D-UPPER(6)` only; fixed-case
  certificate work then stops.
- No additional Ringel selector, predecessor-half selector retuning, SAT seed,
  proof shrinking, broad seven-to-ten atlas, or full Ferrers formalization is
  authorized by this ticket.

## Resolution

Resolved on 2026-08-15 with no load-bearing destination dependency changed.
All four task/result contracts validate, and root replay reproduced the
decorated-atlas report and checker byte-for-byte.

1. `TRANSITION-LAND`: `compatibleRightExtensionLink_hasPermutation` and
   `compatibleRightExtensionLinks_connectorBlockLaw` compile.  They prove the
   permutation representation, common connector intervals, exact four-word
   law, and one-directional within-block changes.  No theorem says that the
   first link and connector blocks determine the second link.
2. `DECORATED-NOT-WELL-POSED`: all 1,550 admitted through-six two-step cells
   satisfy the formal transition law, but 195 of 310 first-link/connector-block
   classes contain multiple second-link permutations.  The proposed
   source-faithful left/right and inner/outer spike labels first become
   nonvacuous at seven total points, so this is a sample ceiling rather than a
   refutation of that decoration.
3. `SUK-CHAIN-ONLY`: the exact conditional binomial recurrence on a total
   `SukRegionLE` chain and the global-selection-to-certificate consumer
   compile.  Transitivity and anchor semantics remain explicit producers, and
   anchor-plus-each-triple convexity is not yet promoted to convexity of the
   whole anchor-extended selected set.  Thus the global adjacent selection is
   still open.
4. `ES6-SOURCE-NEEDED`: the local primary paper supports an exact architecture
   from the eight `E*` four-sign patterns through `OneChangeOn`, a six-point
   weak/strong convexity bridge, the 17-vertex finite theorem, the existing
   planar presentation, and existing sharpness.  The referenced Isabelle/HOL
   theories and proof artifacts are absent locally.  No finite theorem or
   certificate was admitted.

**Net proof movement:** neutral.  **Evidence:** two compiling Lean modules,
checked bounded computation, and a checked source/architecture audit.
**Difficulty displaced:** none demonstrably smaller; the open obligations are
a predictive source-backed transition consumer, Suk's geometric/full-cap and
adjacent-selection producers, and a kernel-checked 17-vertex theorem.
**Counterfactual:** these outcomes change allocation by rejecting connector
blocks as a deterministic recurrence, preserving only a minimal seven-point
label test, and preferring retrieval of the existing exact-six formal source
over rebuilding its enumeration.  They do not reduce the estimated remaining
exact-conjecture proof work.  **Planning consequence:** retain all three
mechanisms as gated infrastructure; close this execution card and do not open
a broad seven-point atlas, asymptotic Suk assembly, or new exact-six certificate
engineering before the named producer/source gates.

## Four-bridge follow-up resolution

The approved follow-up slate completed on 2026-08-15 with two
proof-producing slots and two information-gathering slots.

1. `LABEL-LOSS`: the unchanged seven-point descriptor is exactly refuted by
   rational configurations.  The collision preserves all connector words,
   all 18 hidden core-link bits, and the source vertical order while reversing
   the left/right role; only `chi 4 5 6` differs.  The pairwise descendant is
   expired, and n=8 through n=10 remained unopened.
2. `CUTSEM-DIFFUSE`: independent provenance replays of the accepted REPAIR and
   balanced `3+3` contradictions expose no compact reusable semantic join.
   REPAIR does not use its repair terminal, while the `3+3` stable boundary has
   2,104 joins across 2,013 patterns.  No candidate was frozen and no Lean
   premise was added.
3. `SUK-GEOMETRY-LAND`: `SukRegionGeometry` now produces the exact anchor
   semantics and transitive two-color law from raw support-region geometry.
   `sukRegionGeometry_to_fullAnchoredCap` proves full anchor-extended convexity,
   and the adjacent-chain adapter compiles.  This closes the named local
   geometry/full-cap dependency in `FP-15.B`.
4. `ES6-SOURCE-STOP`: the official archive is safe and exactly inventoried,
   but contains checked cases only through five, dormant `6/17` generator
   input, and no license, session root, generated formula, proof, or final
   six-point theorem.  The source-import child is suspended.

**Net proof movement:** forward only on the scoped Suk dependency; neutral on
the exact destination.  **Evidence:** a compiling Lean theorem suite, two
independently checked computations, and an exact source audit.  **Difficulty
displaced:** the Suk route no longer assumes local semantics, transitivity, or
full-cap promotion; its remaining global adjacent-chain/cardinality producer
is strictly downstream.  The other three obligations are not smaller.
**Counterfactual:** knowing these results would have avoided the n=8--10
pairwise tests, further trace mining, and attempted import from the incomplete
archive, while still scheduling the Suk theorem.  **Planning consequence:**
promote `FP-15.B` to its global adjacent-pool gate, expire the tested `FP-13`
descriptor, require route review for correlated `FP-02/FP-05`, and suspend
`FP-16`.  No load-bearing ledger edge changed.
