consumer ladder into `D-HALF`, `D-DECOMP`, or `D-NORMALIZE`, now that the

## Decision criteria

- `POST-V3-CARD-ADMISSIBLE`: at least one literal card satisfies every common
  field, survives root source/interface checking and the bounded reciprocal

  not execution or portfolio promotion.

  defect. Preserve the narrow negative scope without claiming source or
  mechanism exhaustion.

## Common card-admission contract

Every proposed card must state:

1. its stable mechanism family and precise model tier;
2. the exact structural theorem or falsifier, preferably as a Lean-shaped
   declaration;
3. the named intermediary bridge and load-bearing consumer;
4. a consumer ladder `structural theorem -> intermediary -> ledger edge` with
   at most one speculative arrow beyond the proposed theorem;
5. all producer obligations and why the premise is not target-equivalent;
6. the strongest relevant prior negative evidence and failure genome;
7. the cheapest prospective falsifier, with fixed outcome branches and a
   bounded resource envelope;
8. source provenance, including a primary source and retrieval date for any
   imported mathematical mechanism; and
9. the strict predeclared measure that a compiling success would decrease.

A finite example, source analogy, conditional interface, SAT/UNSAT proposal,
or appealing invariant is proposal-level search information.  It does not
enter the portfolio or become proof movement without the ordinary admission
and route decision.
