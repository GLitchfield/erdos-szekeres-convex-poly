# Ticket 0048: admit or reject Batch 006 frontier ideas

## Question

Which of the five Batch 006 proposals should be merged into an existing stable
mechanism family, admitted as a new frontier entry, retained only in the idea
ledger, or expired before the next execution slate is selected?

## Decision criteria

Each proposal must have a precise model tier, named destination consumer,
scope ceiling, missing generalization bridge, bounded discriminating gate,
outcome branches, and an expiry or revival rule. Shared mathematical objects,
unresolved producers, or recorded counterexamples determine correlation even
when a proposal uses new vocabulary. A finite gate may justify portfolio
membership, but it does not constitute proof movement or revive a suspended
uniform parent.

New main entries are admitted conservatively. A proposal is merged when its
consumer and failure mode are already owned by an existing stable family. It
remains ledger-only when its key premise is strategically equivalent to the
target, its transfer is undefined, or its first gate cannot distinguish the
proposed mechanism. Expiry requires a decisive contradiction or duplication,
not merely a low prior probability.

This ticket resolves portfolio disposition only. It does not select or approve
an execution slate. Any addition, revival, or material route redirection must
be checked by the bounded dual-provider adversarial review before integration.

## Resolution

Outcome: `BATCH006-PORTFOLIO-ADMITTED`, limited to two conservative
existing-family child merges.

The exact dual-provider command was launched once. Codex Sol completed a
high-confidence `partially_supported` review. Claude Fable returned HTTP 429
with zero input and output tokens, so it supplied no mathematical opinion and
the harness stopped before reciprocal critique. Consistent with Ticket 0046,
this operational limitation is recorded rather than treated as a negative
vote. It does not support a new main route or a parent revival. The surviving
review and root audit agree on the following conservative reconciliation:

1. **B006-I01: merge into `FP-01.A`, cross-reference `FP-14.B`.** The exact
   child gate is to truth-table one representative of each of eight frozen
   direct-predicate schemas and verify all six unit/index maps, then only on
   agreement prove one selected-cube semantic implication consumed by the
   compiling checkpoint leaf. A semantic mismatch expires this interface; an
   operational stop preserves generic facts but authorizes no more LRAT work.
   Its ceiling is one frozen Fin11 cube. It does not prove the other 63 cubes,
   restriction/relabeling, overlap synchronization, or the uniform half-support
   producer, and it does not revive suspended `FP-01`.
2. **B006-I02: ledger-only.** Its smallest missing admission fact is an
   independently motivated collision-to-weak-polygon theorem explaining why
   alpha multiplicities are redundant. Support injectivity by itself is the
   cardinality-producing step rather than a demonstrated reduction of it.
3. **B006-I03: ledger-only.** It first needs one positive realizable control
   showing that the original endpoint-downset family is closed under Boolean
   median without taking a median hull. It inherits the endpoint-family
   negative streak and does not reset that evidence by vocabulary.
4. **B006-I04: ledger-only.** Before any polymorphism test, a checked
   bidirectional source/CSP translation must preserve every global provenance
   and packing relation. Otherwise the eight-cycle CSP may be a weakened or
   accidentally empty terminal. The required correlated `FP-02`/`FP-05` review
   is complete, but the proposal is not yet an admitted child.
5. **B006-I05: merge as gated child `FP-09.A`.** Independently enumerate exact
   rational chord arrangements and every source-valid corner peeling on fresh
   `k=9` and `k=10` controls, capped at 250,000 chambers per arrangement.
   Endpoint-first failure and target-extraction failure must remain separate.
   One exhaustive rational counterexample expires this refinement without
   retuning; finite survival is search information until a uniform theorem and
   abstract `OneChangeOn` transfer compile.

No new top-level `FP-NN` entry is added, no existing parent changes state, and
no execution slate is selected.

### Final proof-distance accounting

- **Net proof movement:** neutral.

- **Planning consequence:** retain the two admitted gated children, keep the

  temporary-slate selection.
- **Load-bearing ledger:** no edge changed.
