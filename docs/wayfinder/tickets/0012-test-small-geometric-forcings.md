## Question

Do any small, independently valid consequences of planar realizability change
the combinatorics of a named exact-route obstruction beyond the already proved
four-point `OneChangeOn` law?

The purpose is causal discrimination, not a search for progressively stronger
assumptions. A forcing matters only if its validity for sweep-realizable
colorings is proved or exactly certified and it closes or strictly reduces a
named obstruction. A smaller model count, finite survival, or conditional
consumer is not by itself forward proof movement.

## Selected gates

1. Remove only `OneChangeOn` from three existing exact formulas to measure its
   causal role.
2. Authenticate and test the first nontrivial local stretchability filter: the
   nonrealizable uniform rank-three nine-element class.
3. Prove or refute the existing `FP-15.B` producer from two adjacent Suk
   support-region chains to the arrangement-cell and pair-support certificate.
4. Replace the Layer-F endpoint split shield by the source-side condition that
   each changed carrier is internal to a proper source split, and prove the
   resulting persistence implication if it is genuinely weaker.

The first and second gates are diagnostic. The third is a supporting
quantitative theorem. The fourth can reduce `FP-04` but does not close
`D-NORMALIZE` without a uniform mutation-choice producer.

## Decision criteria

- Freeze every forcing before its outcome is inspected.
- Test whether it is already implied by `OneChangeOn` or restates an existing
  shield before running a large computation.
- Require exact rational controls for any claimed geometric refutation.
- Require an independent clause or certificate checker for every SAT/UNSAT
  result.
- Do not use published nonrealizability as a Lean premise. A local-minor
  forcing enters a formal theorem only after a compiling realizability bridge.
- Stop a forcing family after one checked survivor shows that it does not
  change the named conclusion.
- Do not retune a failed local forcing on its counterexample during this
  ticket.

## Candidate outcomes

1. One forcing compiles and closes or strictly reduces its named obstruction;
   retain only that mechanism for a follow-up slate.
2. A forcing filters models but a checked survivor remains; record it as
   diagnostic and expire that application.
3. A frozen forcing is refuted by an exact rational configuration; retain the
   counterexample and expire the statement.
4. The forcing duplicates `OneChangeOn`, an existing certificate field, or the
   desired conclusion; remove it from the execution frontier.
5. Data, proof, or resource admission fails; record an operational stop with no
   mathematical inference.

## Resolution

The four frozen gates are complete.

1. **`OC-ESSENTIAL` (diagnostic).**  Removing only the `OneChangeOn` clauses
   makes the exact Fin10 singleton-fan, packed `3+3`, and collision-repair
   formulas satisfiable.  Independent semantic replay finds many four-point
   OneChange violations in every model.  This confirms that the already
   formalized geometric law is causally load-bearing, but proves no new
   theorem.
2. **`MINOR-LAND` (diagnostic).**  An authenticated Ringel nonstretchable
   rank-three nine-element class occurs in exactly 60 restrictions of the
   admitted relaxed state-567 model.  This rules out that model as a realizable
   configuration only after a formal realizability-to-minor bridge is supplied;
   no load-bearing dependency changed.
3. **`SUPPORT-LAND` (local forward result).**  The compiling theorem
   `SukAdjacentSupportData.toCertificate` derives the arrangement-cell and
   opposite-pair support certificate from two actual adjacent support-region
   chains and their anchor-extended caps.  The proof uses explicit
   Grassmann--Pluecker sign identities and assumes none of its certificate
   fields.  This closes the named local producer edge in `FP-15.B`, but not the
   global chain-selection, positive-fraction, recurrence, or asymptotic layers.
4. **`CIRCUIT-LOCAL` (infrastructure).**
   `HasDeepBelowSplit.persists_of_singleMutation_internal` and its
   cardinality-restricted source-side consumer compile.  No theorem produces
   the source condition uniformly.  The frozen Fin17 test is already
   unit-inconsistent at its endpoint-activation layer, so it supplies no
   evidence for the global mutation producer.

The slate therefore moves one supporting quantitative edge and no
load-bearing exact-conjecture edge.  The OneChange and nine-minor results are
retained as negative/search information, and the local circuit theorem is
retained as reusable infrastructure without promoting `FP-04`.
