Date: 2026-08-10

Status: Compiling infrastructure; replacement route modified

## Question

After the two-anchor rooted-circuit route failed, what is the smallest sound
formal object for studying sweep-realizable configurations outside the
recursively decomposable class?

## Compiling result

`DecompositionPrime.lean` defines a proper deep-below split, a
decomposition-prime support, and deletion-minimal indecomposability. Lean
proves `exists_deletionMinimal_indec_prime`: every nonempty indecomposable
finite support contains a deletion-minimal indecomposable subset, and that
subset has cardinality at least two and admits no proper deep-below split.

This theorem is useful infrastructure only. At the exact cardinality
`2^(k-2)+1`, a smaller decomposable subset cannot by itself invoke the exact
E3 threshold. Any successful Layer-F theorem must also supply a shelling,
normalization, weighted recurrence, or target-reflecting consumer.

## Bounded proposal evidence

The read-only specialist reported an exhaustive abstract census with no prime
signotopes through five vertices and 190 root-prime colorings among 908
six-vertex signotopes. It also reported an explicit rational eight-point
target-free prime for which no one-vertex deletion remains prime, while a
two-vertex deletion does. These numbers and witnesses were produced in memory
without a committed producer/certificate/independent checker and are therefore
not completed results.

The modified next conjecture is a one-or-two deletion theorem for realizable
prime supports, followed by a noncircular local binary pin coding. The first
bounded gate is an independently replayed `n=7` signotope census followed by
rational classification of every abstract failure. The route expires on a
rational target-free prime with no prime subconfiguration after deleting at
most two vertices, or if surviving deletion steps admit no local injective
coding.

Two backups remain proposal-level: uncrossing incompatible deep splits via a
four-corner strong set, and target-free realizable single-triple mutation to a
decomposable coloring. Neither currently has a proved exact-threshold
consumer.
