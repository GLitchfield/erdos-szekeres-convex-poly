# R18 three separated source-child sites in a single-run shadow

Unregistered, unchecked preparation; same axis-two ROOT-NF route. Proposed
public name ctx_single_run_shadow_of_three_separated_sites_chain_ge_two.
Arbitrary raw context and indexed old level N. Three distinguished sites
x<l<y<r<z lie between two old N markers. Each distinguished site is either
still a child or replaced by a chosen-side positive level smaller than N;
all such new indexed sides are chosen. With at most one physical child run,
every retained child point has chosen chain extension at least two.

Proof route: reuse the accepted raw one-run interval argument from R18's
single-run normalization. A child run cannot cross l or r. A leaf point left
of l forces middle site y to be younger, followed outward by old marker r;
a point between l/r forces younger left/right sites x/z; a point right of r
uses younger y followed outward left by old l. Two-level sublist folds use
the already accepted R15 local hTwoLeft/hTwoRight argument from
ctx_part_bound_zero_of_near_saturated_sibling_and_younger_boundary_marker.

No global, k, maximum-value, WF or positivity hypothesis is needed beyond
the explicitly constrained tags/indices. Empty child sets are vacuous.
The positive leaf witness would supply these shadow hypotheses for a root
with three separated original runs. Extraction of three run starts and the
ROOT-NF equality remain separate obligations. No compiler or theorem credit.
