# A cap-three root is a lower spine with cup flanks and one possible apex

2026-10-03 20:29UTC. Hand corollary of accepted DS endpoint insertion
theorems, not a new compiling R24 theorem or proof of G.

For HasDeepBelowSplit chi L U with no4cap on L union U, choose l<r in L.
There is at most one U point strictly between l,r: two such points form a
2cap, and adjoining the lower endpoints produces a4cap. The U points before
l form a cup, since any3cap there can be extended on the right by l to4cap.
The U points after r form a cup by the left-endpoint argument. Thus at the
extremes of a nonsingleton L, U is a left cup flank, at most one interior
point, and a right cup flank. If L is singleton, U has just the two cup
flanks. These implications need only the deep-split rule and cap exclusion,
not OneChange or decomposability of either child.

This is a geometric interpretation of the inherited minus1/minus2 endpoint
constraints. It is not an interval-root normal form: the lower child can
still have gaps, and the upper interior point can lie inside one of them.
The lower-spine viewpoint is a possible different abstraction from direct
Baek-label truncation, which the four-point observation refutes.

Let R bound the parent cup and strong ranks, let b be the actual upper cap
rank, and let dL,d be the minimum ordered cup-block counts of L and parent.
Strong coupling gives cup(L)<=R-b. Cap4 avoidance only gives
strong(L)<=cup(L)+1, so lowering the cup bound does not lower the strong
bound by the same amount. A classical clear-block upper F would control
that lower child without a new strong hypothesis; F itself is still open
in the intermediate range.

For a two-cap upper child (a pure cup), the proposed arithmetic is simple:
F(R-2,3,dL)+R=G(R,dL+1). If its points create no extra parent cup block,
they must instead fit as a few endpoints of existing blocks, so a full R
charge is inappropriate. With upper cap rank3, the possible interior point
can align with an existing lower block boundary or create a new one. The
budget must distinguish those two cases and preserve the endpoint trace.
This note does not establish that budget or silently identify d-dL with an
independent upper block count.

The obstruction remains one of joint endpoint information. Scalar ranks,
cup-block counts and a successful whole-zone lower construction do not yet
pay for every root. No narrowed trial or larger census is commissioned on
this root route; keep G and classical F upper obligations open.
